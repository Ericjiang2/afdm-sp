## Design (frozen)
- F0 + CSTATE as in the ρ sweep (res-20261119-06). Only max_edits changes. The cap-only gate passed: identical first two decisions and
  stopping reasons across caps on 14 archived triggers.
- Arms: CD2/CD4/CD6 (`CODELAY`) and A2-2/A2-6 (scalar).
- Grid: ρ ∈ {.15, .20, .30} × U/G × 3–10 dB.
- **Fresh frames** 6400–6799 (400): 24,000 keys.
- **Primary (§7).** g(ρ) = rescue(CD4) − rescue(CD2) on triggered keys, paired and pooled.
  - ρ = .15 uses 6–8 dB.
  - The ρ = .30 comparator uses **4.5/5.0 dB**. These cells were chosen by archived rescue headroom (.66/.93), because at 6–8 dB
    ρ = .30 had 42 triggers, all already rescued.
  - Labels: direction = g(.15) lower bound > 0 and g(.15) − g(.30) lower bound > 0; practical size 3 pp; matched equivalence ±3 pp.
  - Frame-cluster bootstrap, 20,000 draws.

## Explanation (untested; confidence M)
- **Budget runs out only at low pilot fraction.** Two edits run out when F0 misses many delays, which happens at low pilot fraction.
  With exact sum-mode scoring, CODELAY spends the extra edits on true delays. The scalar spends them mostly on phantoms, consistent with
  the spectral principle (res-20261119-03).
- **The collapse is only partly a budget artefact.** The extra edits buy about 0.6–0.9 dB of crossing at ρ ≤ .20. The system optimum
  stays at ρ = .30 (5.15 dB), so most of the low-ρ loss is the pilot bootstrap itself.
- **Design rule** (as observed, not derived): set the edit budget to about 4 at low pilot fraction. Two are enough at ρ ≥ .3.
