## Bounds (genie delays, P = 8 known, unit path-power constraint)
- **MCRB.** The data is random and averaged out, with the exact coded-data covariance. It is the only one of these four that
  is a strict lower bound for the real model. It is also the primary bound. Under the constraint (Gorman–Hero) it is
  0.18–0.19 dB below the unconstrained one.
- **DA.** The true data is known, frame by frame. It is .04–.05 dB above the MCRB.
- **PI.** Pilot-only information, with the data treated as Gaussian interference of frozen covariance. It is an auxiliary
  reference and carries no bound guarantee.
- **GD.** Full Slepian–Bangs for Gaussian data, computed on frames 0..99. Also auxiliary.
- Van Trees is not used, because the generator priors violate its regularity conditions (protocol §1.2).

- Under the wording rule (protocol §5), no SNR point supports "within 1 dB" or "within 3 dB of the MCRB" for either estimator.
- B's final NMSE is almost flat in SNR: about −16 dB at ρ .3 and −20 to −21 dB at ρ .5. The gap therefore widens with SNR.
- The MCRB itself barely depends on ρ. It is close to the white-input value 23σ²/(2N).
- **Descriptive (not labelled; I1 items were added after the smoke):**
  - *Error decomposition.* Different delays are orthogonal, so the NMSE splits exactly into missed-delay energy plus the
    error on the found delays. At 6.5–8 dB, missed delays account for 81–86% of B's mean NMSE at ρ .3 and 59–73% at
    ρ .5. The rest is 2.4–3.0 dB (ρ .3) and 0.9–2.4 dB (ρ .5) above the MCRB. The MCRB still counts all 8 paths, so that comparison is approximate.
    At 4.5 dB the missed share is 23–40% and the rest is 4.4–5.4 dB above the MCRB.
  - *Where the delay support comes from.* F0's delay support is fixed at the pilot stage (missed energy identical for
    F0-pilot and F0-final). Only the CSTATE branch changes it. On triggered frames, B lowers the NMSE relative to F0 in
    79–100% of frames (median −0.3 to −12.6 dB per SNR point).
  - *Pilot stage.* `r0_ce_gains` are shrunk by design. `time_refine` with mu = 0 solves (BᴴB + Γ + σ²I)h = Bᴴy, where Γ is
    the data variance. The NMSE is therefore about −3 dB (ρ .3) or −6 dB (ρ .5) at every SNR. An unbiased pilot-only LS
    refit on the same geometry (F0-pilot-LS) is 3.8–6.1 dB above PI.
  - *Pilot-information gap.* PI sits 5.9–10.6 dB above the MCRB. This is the price of treating the superimposed data as
    interference. GD, which adds the blind second-order information, is 1.3–3.0 dB below PI.
  - *Stratified by decoding.* On frames that B decodes, the gap is 3.7–11.5 dB. On failed frames it is 5.3–13.5 dB.
    The per-frame median ratio is 3.5–10.5 dB. Conditioning on success removes the bound guarantee, so these are descriptive.

## Interpretation (scoped)
Measured against a strict lower bound (the constrained MCRB with genie delays), the channel estimate of the deployable
receiver is not near-efficient. Its final NMSE has a floor of about −16 dB at ρ .3 and −20 dB at ρ .5. The post hoc
decomposition says the floor is mostly weak paths whose delays are never found. The genie-delay bound contains no term for
that error. On the delays it does find, the estimate is about 1–3 dB above the MCRB at high SNR at ρ .5; at ρ .3 a
4.5–5.3 dB refit residual remains even with true delays ([res-20261016-03](res-20261016-03.md); revised 2026-10-16,
see Revision log). This is the channel-side
counterpart of P1's 0.12–0.55 dB FER gap to genie CSI. The missed paths carry little energy and cost little in FER.
For the paper, the third tier reads as follows. MCRB is a lower reference for channel MSE. F0+CSTATE does not attain it.
The reason is support acquisition (pilot-stage delay detection), not the refit. The decomposition is descriptive and
was not pre-registered.

## Limits
- Genie delays and known P. The bound has no term for delay detection, which is where most of the gap is.
- Only the MCRB is strict for this model. DA, PI and GD are descriptive references. ECRB averages conditional CRBs;
  it is not a Bayesian bound for every estimator (a Weiss–Weinstein bound would be needed).
- CRBs constrain estimators that are locally unbiased for H. The receiver's gains are regularized: this is explicit in
  `r0_ce_gains`, and the joint gain prior is 1/P.
- The NMSE floor and the missed-energy share are post hoc descriptions (I1 was added after seeing the smoke), not tested
  hypotheses.
- Gate MC standard errors were defined as the spread of the relative error. That understates the expected MC error of a
  matrix norm, so the 3% floor was the binding tolerance. G5 and G5b give the 1% anchors.
- Reused P1 frames (500 of 2000), one modulation, code and N, sigma2 known, the same channel law as P1.
