## Interpretation
1. **The Berkson over-shrinkage reading is refuted for this pipeline.** Removing Γ from the gain estimate never helps: FER
   triples under full Γ and rises under scalar Γ. With no uncertainty term anywhere (Z), FER is six times the scalar arm's.
   The res-20261018-02 toy used oracle support with well-conditioned atoms, where bias dominates; the real pipeline selects
   about 22 atoms, including near-collinear co-delay ones, where variance dominates.
2. **The decoder-uncertainty term works as a regulariser, and the isotropic form is the best one tested.** Ordering:
   scalar (.043) < full Γ (.116) < none (.254). This agrees with E-3c, where adding Σv·I to full Γ recovers 92% of its gap.
   The full Γ removes part of the regularisation on the difference directions of near-collinear atoms (eigenvalue Σv(1 − |D_N|)),
   and those are the directions where noise is amplified.
3. **Lower NMSE does not imply lower FER.** D-ls0 has lower mean NMSE than D-sbl but higher FER. Conservative, regularised
   gains decode better when the detector takes the gains as fixed.
4. **Updated synthesis across E-1 … GSO (post hoc, scoped).** The coherent structure of Γ helps discrete support decisions in a
   fixed-slot search, because it removes the phantom-duplicate incentive (E-1/E-2). For estimating the gains, the
   uncertainty should enter as isotropic regularisation; the coherent structure weakens it exactly on near-collinear
   directions (E-3, E-3c, GSO). CSTATE uses Γ only in the decision, and the detector re-fits the gains jointly. This
   decision/estimation split is now consistent with every result in this sequence. It is not yet a prospectively
   confirmed rule.

## Limits
- One SBL receiver, on-grid, 100 EM iterations (most C/Z runs hit the cap); ρ .3, U/G, 6–11 dB, 100 frames; the keys reuse
  E-3/E-3c frames, so this is not an independent confirmation sample.
- LS0 uses ridge σ² only; no intermediate ridge levels were tested.
- The synthesis in item 4 is post hoc. A pre-registered test on fresh frames, including CSTATE with an isotropic-only
  gain step, would be needed before it becomes a paper claim.

## Correction note (2026-10-18, explicit; original text above unchanged)
Interpretation item 4 (the post hoc decision/estimation split) was tested prospectively on fresh frames in
[res-20261018-04](res-20261018-04.md). **Its estimation half is refuted.** With the atoms fixed, isotropic-ridge gains are
worse than full-Γ gains: S-H against S-C +.040 FER, and on A2 paths −.029 rescue. Its decision half replicated (+.18). The
reading in item 2, that full Γ is weaker "mainly because it under-regularises near-collinear difference directions", is
therefore also not supported as a description of gain estimation. This card's pre-registered VARIANCE-SUPPORTED verdict
(Γ-free LS gains are worse than SBL gains) stands.
