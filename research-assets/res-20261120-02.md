## Interpretation (untested beyond what is stated)
1. **H** — The self-consistency result survives genuine doublets in one respect: deleting Γ breeds no singleton phantoms (0 vs 0; scalar 333).
2. **M** — With true close doublets, `CODELAY` may keep a small edge over Γ = 0. Where the two differ, the CD-only two-atom frames
   show Z leaving 13.7% of the doublet's energy unfitted, against 0.8% for CD. The FER direction (+1.0 pp, 10 : 4) agrees, but 600
   frames cannot separate it from zero. Astra's local argument predicted Z ≥ CD in occupancy; the observed sign is the opposite.
3. **M** — In both MIXED8 tests the exact co-delay block sits at a sweet spot: adding a floor (U, res-20261119-04) and removing the
   block (Z, here) each cost about 1 pp FER and some doublet occupancy. Two inconclusive results; not a finding.
4. **H** — A co-delay ban (A2g) is not a substitute: it fails 55% of frames because it cannot represent genuine doublets.

## Limits
One operating point (ρ .5, 7 dB), 600 frames; P-F's interval is too wide to separate a 1 pp difference from zero. Doublet
occupancy is not ray recovery (no Doppler matching tolerance frozen).

## Consequence (proposal; Eric decides)
- Together with res-20261120-01: Γ = 0 is equivalent on P1 and phantom-free on MIXED8, but its FER on genuine doublets is unresolved
  and leans worse. `CODELAY` can be kept as the default self-consistent member with a stated, untested possible advantage when true
  close co-delay pairs exist. A larger MIXED8 cohort (≈ 2,400 frames, about 1 core-hour) would resolve P-F.
