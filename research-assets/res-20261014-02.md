## Interpretation (scoped)
DDST does what it was designed to do at the estimator: the round-1 pilot-only channel error drops 6–28% (L8 .115 vs .160).
The detector pays more than that: round-1 decoder-input logloss rises at every L (L8 .440 vs .400; L32 .702 vs .664), even
with an oracle channel (.486 vs .446). The nulled subspace must be large (r_90 ~ .33–.66 N, res-20261014-01), so the
distortion removes data information that the screen's bounds treated as free. A transmitter without delay feedback performs
the same as the genie one (R10 .824 vs .842), so delay knowledge is not the binding factor. The DDST-aware receiver is
essential (unaware DDST-N collapses), but awareness does not recover the loss.

## Limits
One channel law (C1 clusters, genie delays, flat DPSS prior), rho .3, two SNRs, R10/T20, r fixed to the screen's r_opt.
Smaller r, other rho, joint (r, rho) design or a different receiver were not tested; the claim is "DDST with the screen's
nulling rank does not move the SP-AFDM cliff in this setting", not "no data-dependent training can help".
