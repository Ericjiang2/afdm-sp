## Calibration and scientific interpretation

- Pooled realized error energy / APP variance **kappa = 2.1491**. Of all hard-symbol errors, **23.17%** occur at declared confidence `v < .1 p_d`. The lowest-variance bin has mean variance .000999 and mean squared error .06318: clear empirical energy miscalibration in this sample.
- Adding an isotropic penalty substantially improves FER under the current finite-budget pipeline. This supports regularization sensitivity; it does not prove the scalar advantage comes uniquely from redundant-direction shrinkage, nor that physical-direction regularization is unchanged.
- The realized-error-profile intervention does not rescue the full arm. Since it is not a correct posterior covariance, the result does **not** rule out posterior mean bias, symbol correlation, pseudocovariance or other model misspecification. Empirical miscalibration exists even though this particular intervention fails.
- No new deployable rule, capacity law or universal full-versus-scalar principle has been established. No F0+CSTATE head-to-head comparison was performed.
