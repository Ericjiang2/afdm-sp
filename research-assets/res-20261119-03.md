## Explanation (the mechanism is now prospectively supported at intervention level; the interpretation below is confidence M)
- **One spectral fact, two opposite failures.**
  - The exact co-delay block u·D has spectrum u·λ(D). It is large on the resolvable (sum) modes and near zero on the sub-resolution
    (difference) modes. The scalar uI flattens it to u.
  - **Discrete support search (CSTATE)** needs the exact penalty on the resolvable modes. Without it, splitting a strong path across
    two near-coincident atoms lowers the penalty, which creates the phantom reward. The scalar fails here. Restoring only the high half
    (C-hi) equals `CODELAY` on every key.
  - **Overcomplete EM-SBL** needs at least the scalar floor on the sub-resolution modes. The exact block leaves them unpenalised and
    fails. Adding u only there recovers most of the gap; adding it on the resolvable half does almost nothing.
  - Γ_U meets both requirements, and it is the only arm tested that avoids both failures.
  - **Theory status** ([Astra, 2026-11-19](../.trellis/tasks/11-19-unified-cov-prospective/research/astra-spectral-proposition-20261119.md);
    proofs not yet checked):
    - **Derived at the objective level.** The split reward depends only on the sum-mode penalty, so C-hi ≡ CD at coalescence. Under a
      fixed prior, the exact block leaves the contrast at prior variance and a floor restores curvature.
    - **Not derived.** The FER ordering in iterative ARD: the mediator (β, γ update or pruning) is unidentified. The SBL-side benefit
      therefore remains an experimental result.
- **Not explained.** In SBL the scalar still beats U2 by 2.6 pp. A candidate explanation: Γ_U penalises the resolvable modes at u·λ
  (λ up to several), above the scalar's u. Untested; cheapest falsifier: cap the resolvable-mode eigenvalues at 1 in EM (a spectral
  floor-and-cap) and compare with R0.
- **Why AFDM matters here** (post hoc, [readouts §4](../.trellis/tasks/09-22-tf-production-gap-audit/research/zero-cost-readouts-20261119.md)).
  The chirp keeps cross-delay uncertainty in Dirichlet sidelobes (≤ 0.82% of u on 1,000 failed frames, against about 65× more at
  the median for a no-chirp counterfactual). The co-delay block is therefore essentially the whole uncertainty structure the principle
  acts on.
