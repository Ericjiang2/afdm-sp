- rho .5: B → true H gap 0.31 dB [0.17, 0.48] (E-ladder) / 0.41 [0.25, 0.61] (E-rho) @FER 1e-2; 0.14 [0.11, 0.17] @1e-1.

- True-H shift matches 10log10((1−r1)/(1−r2)) within .03 dB.
- Failure predictor: same-delay underfit within-SNR AUC .64–.77 (all rho); pass-1 weak-path threshold model weak (AUC ≤ .58).
- PAPR mean +0.42 dB (.5→.3).

## Interpretation (scoped)
Channel-estimation headroom of the production receiver at rho .5 is ≤ ~0.4 dB, of which local Doppler refinement is ≤ ~0.13 dB
(CI touching 0) — consistent with the CRB-regime bound K_r/(2N). The first-order lever is the pilot power fraction: the data-power
gain 10log10((1−r1)/(1−r2)) is realized almost fully down to rho .3, then an acquisition/multiplicity error floor appears (rho .2).
Optimal rho is FER-target dependent (~.3 at 1e-2).

## Limits
One PDP model, N=1024 only, receiver constants tuned at rho .5 (low-rho results conservative), single fresh population (2000
paired frames/cell), equal total-power comparison (PAPR differs), known sigma2. Per-frame mechanism of the low-rho floor not
established. Prior art: SP-OTFS SINR-optimal pilot power ≈ .30–.36 (Mishra et al., IEEE TWC 2022) — rho≈.3 is consistent with
that literature; not a novelty claim by itself. Changing the paper's rho=.5 reference requires explicit user decision.
