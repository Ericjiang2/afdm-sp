## Explanation (untested; confidence M)
- **One spectral fact, two opposite failures.** The exact co-delay block is u·D, with spectrum u·λ(D): large on resolvable (sum)
  modes and near zero on sub-resolution (difference) modes. The scalar uI flattens this spectrum.
  - **Discrete support search (CSTATE).** The scalar under-penalises the coincident sum mode, which creates the phantom reward
    (sentence 2; res-20261118-06).
  - **Overcomplete EM-SBL.** The exact Γ leaves the sub-resolution modes unregularised. Adding u only there recovers the entire gap,
    while adding it on resolvable modes does nothing.

  This is a single-mechanism candidate for skeleton sentence 4.
- **Unified-model candidate.** Γ + u·Π_lo leaves the equal split of an exact duplicate untouched, because that split has no
  sub-resolution component (derived for the coincident pair), so it should keep CODELAY's phantom suppression in CSTATE. It should
  also match or beat the scalar in SBL. Both predictions are **untested**.
- **Diagnostic pattern (observation; interpretation L).** Every rescuing arm keeps about 23–24 co-delay pairs at the output, as the
  scalar does; the full Γ keeps about 5. Regularising sub-resolution modes may let EM spread energy across adjacent co-delay atoms,
  interpolating fractional Doppler, instead of concentrating it.
