## Arms
- **GS-set.** `native_twofft.receive` with the TF_J20_R2 schedule (soft CE, joint gains). Only the refit's delay list is replaced, by
  the true delay set. Dopplers and gains are refit with the production VP objective: Doppler initial values come from acquired
  paths at the same delay, otherwise from a coarse scan. Detector and decoder are unchanged. No CSTATE.
- **GS-multi.** The true delay multiset (headroom A1). All 4000 channels have 8 distinct delays (D2 grid), so it equals GS-set
  up to slot order. The multiplicity step of the planned decomposition is empty for this generator, which the protocol did not anticipate.
- **GS+C (A1, post hoc).** GS-set, then B's own `run_validation._branch(..., 'CSTATE20')` iff the GS-set syndrome ≠ 0.
  Of triggered frames, 75–100% keep the true delay set (mostly 75–85%); CSTATE edits it in the rest.

- At 4.5–6.0 dB, f_set is small or moderate (.02–.79, smallest at ρ .5 around 5.5 dB). There the gap is not mainly support.

## Interpretation (scoped)
- For the channel estimate, the pre-registered test confirms res-20261016-02's post hoc reading in direction. Most of the gap
  to the constrained MCRB at 6.5–8 dB is missed delays (f = .73–.81, 4/4 cells).
- It revises the size of the residual. At ρ .3, with the true delays, the refit is still 4.5–5.3 dB above the MCRB, not 1–3 dB.
  The refit is near the bound (≤ 3 dB) only at ρ .5. At ρ .3 a non-negligible part of the gap sits in the refit (Doppler/gain
  estimation with less pilot power and regularized gains).
- For FER (post hoc), the B → true-H gap is mostly support acquisition, once both arms get the CSTATE continuation. At ρ .5,
  delay acquisition is the whole remaining gap.
- For the paper, the claim reads: "the deployable receiver's residual gap to genie CSI is dominated by delay-support
  acquisition". The NMSE part is pre-registered; the FER attribution is post hoc.

## Limits
- Genie information is the delay list only (GS-set also uses true per-delay energy for slot order). Dopplers are refit from
  acquired paths or a coarse scan, so local minima count as refit error.
- The decomposition's multiplicity step is empty: the D2 grid gives 8 distinct delays in every P1 channel.
- The CRB bound applies only to locally unbiased estimators; the gains are regularized (ridge plus detector prior 1/P).
- Everything about FER attribution (A1, the F0 check) was decided after seeing the main-run FER.
- Same frames, code, N, QPSK, known σ² and channel law as P1. NMSE uses 500 frames.
