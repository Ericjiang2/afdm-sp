## Interpretation
1. **E-1 replicates on new frames** (D1). CSTATE's gain is not an extra round. Swaps carry it. Removing the off-diagonal
   covariance with swaps on costs more than the whole geometry gain.
2. **Without true same-delay paths, the covariance effect is entirely phantom-atom suppression** (D2 PHANTOM-FULL). Banning
   co-delay atoms gives the scalar model exactly the full-covariance result. The "full Γ also ranks true delays better"
   alternative is refuted on this channel class.
3. **With true same-delay rays, that ban removes the whole gain, and the full covariance keeps it** (D3 PRINCIPLE). In C the
   rescue is carried by co-delay atoms. The guarded arms fall to the do-nothing level (.015–.016), A1 stays at .126, and the
   ban costs nothing on R.
4. **Only the full covariance is best, or tied for best, in both classes.**
   - The co-delay ban loses .110 on C.
   - The theorem-derived near-duplicate ban (A2n) ties on C but loses .033 on R. Under the scalar model, phantom co-delay atoms
     with |D_N| < .5 are still accepted, in 43% of units, and still harm decoding.
   - The unguarded scalar model loses on both classes.
   - Read together: the data-aided support decision hinges on co-delay atoms, and the full projected covariance gets that decision
     right in both regimes tested, while each fixed rule fails in one of them.

## Limits
- **Clusters are not solved.** On C, F0 FER stays at .89–.99 over 6–20 dB, as in res-20261008-02, and A1 rescues only .126 of
  triggered units, so the overall FER stays high. No arm recovers the true tap-delay set. C shows the decision mechanism, not a
  working cluster receiver; the slot cap (8) and the one-Doppler-per-path receiver model bound what any arm can do.
- **The census cannot separate real from phantom inside C**: 95% of taps carry ≥ 2 rays, so landing on a multi-ray tap is near
  base rate (A1 .9996, A2 .983). Within C, the principle evidence is the rescue contrast (D3), not the census.
- Rescue rates are conditional on F0 failure and do not transfer to full-frame FER.
- One receiver family, one LDPC code, QPSK, N = 1024. One cluster law (Jakes-4), with SNR grids differing by family. A2n uses a
  single threshold.
- The theorem's |D_N| identifies the dominant failure (near duplicates) but does not by itself predict the moderate-separation
  phantoms that the scalar model accepts on R (G_n .815). That needs an extension of the restricted theorem.
- No method-novelty claim. Decoder-aided support re-acquisition exists (arXiv:2501.15935, arXiv:2607.18881). Whether
  "scalar data-uncertainty models create phantom co-delay atoms; the full projected covariance resolves the co-delay decision"
  is new against errors-in-variables sparse regression and soft-data EM channel estimation is **not yet checked**.
