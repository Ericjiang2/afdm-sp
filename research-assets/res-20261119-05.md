## Design (frozen)
- E-3c SBL pipeline, 100 iterations, U/G × ρ .3 × 6–11 dB. **Fresh frames 4800–4999** (2,400 keys, 200 clusters). The 38 unpublished
  rows of the stopped attempt (4600–4605) were never read.
- **Arms.**
  - R0 (scalar) and U2 (Γ + u·Π_lo with Γ_U refinement).
  - **K′** = cap_u(Γ) + u·Π_lo in EM, with U2's refinement. cap_u(Γ) is a global spectral cap at u of the full exact Γ, so U2 − K′ is
    the part of the spectrum above u.
  - **M** = U2's EM output followed by R0's scalar refinement.
  - KM′ = K′'s EM output followed by scalar refinement (secondary).
- **Verdict per arm.** Paired contrasts Δ_X − t·G, where Δ_X = FER(U2) − FER(X) and t = .5 / .25, judged on 97.5% intervals:
  - lower bound > 0 at t = .5 → recovers at least half;
  - upper bound < 0 at t = .25 → recovers less than a quarter;
  - otherwise → uncertain.

## Explanation (untested; confidence M)
- **Both factors contribute, and together they account for the whole gap.**
  - Capping the sum-mode excess in EM brings β, convergence and co-delay pair counts back to the scalar's values.
  - The refinement covariance contributes separately.
  - Neither alone is shown to reach half. With both changed (KM′), the scalar is matched.
  - The point estimates suggest roughly additive, overlapping contributions (κ .66 and .52; interaction CI contains 0).
- **Reading for the principle.** EM-SBL appears to need the co-delay spectrum near u on all modes: a floor on the difference modes
  and no excess on the sum modes. Discrete exchange needs the exact sum mode. This would explain why the scalar does well in SBL: its
  spectrum meets both EM needs and its refinement is scalar. This rests on a secondary readout; it is not a frozen verdict.
- **Cheapest prospective falsifier.** Make KM′ against R0 the primary on fresh frames, using an equivalence margin (for example
  ±0.5 pp). It needs a new pre-registration.
