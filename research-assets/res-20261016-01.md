## Observation
- **Reproduction in the paper's own regime** (N = 512, P = 3, uncoded, M = 8). The implementation equals the printed
  equations to 1e-11.
  - With the printed constants (eq. 29, delta = .1 on the new message), BER is 1.2–5.5x the paper's Fig. 4a read
    values: G-Z3 holds at 2/6 points and G-Z1 fails. **The printed constants do not reproduce.**
  - Reading delta as the weight of the old message (.9 on the new) gives ratios 1.03–1.71 at 6/6 points. This was a
    diagnostic chosen after seeing data. It is not a passed reproduction, and the authors have been asked.
  - Eq. 5 matches the time model only with the input-index / sign-of-alpha convention.
- **Port to the P1 channels** (fractional Doppler, delays up to 102, P = 8 known; one impulse pilot fits because

  - delta .1 was the better reading in every cell.

## Interpretation (scoped)
The only published SP-AFDM joint estimation/detection receiver we could find was designed for small integer
delay-Doppler grids (M_p = 9, P = 3, about 89% pilot energy, uncoded). Its pilot-spacing rule leaves one pilot when the
delay spread is large. Its path selection assumes on-grid Doppler. On an 8-path channel with fractional Doppler and
delays up to 102, it does not reach FER 1e-2 by 8 dB, even with our stronger selection. F0+CSTATE reaches that FER at
5.1–6.3 dB. The comparison shows that the regime of the SP-AFDM literature does not carry over to this channel class.
It does not show that the receiver of Zheng et al. is poor in its own regime.

## Limits

- 1000 frames per point. The gains are censored lower bounds. Z-turbo, a coded-feedback variant, was not run.

## Correction note (2026-10-19, user decision "按显式更正规则改掉"; the Limits text above is kept unchanged)
- **The Limits sentence "P is given to the Zheng variants (the paper's assumption); our receivers do not use it" is
  incorrect.** Our receivers cap the path count at 8, which equals the generator's true path count:
  - The F0 pilot stage (`native_twofft.receive` → `twofft.time_refine(..., max_paths=8)`) keeps the 8 strongest delays
    of the OMP nominations.
  - CSTATE then works on those 8 fixed slots.
  - In the E-2 family R rows (P1 generator, 8052 triggered units), F0's slot count is 8 in every unit, and the true path
    count is also 8 in every unit.
  F0, B and the F1 front end therefore operate with P known as a cap.
- **Corrected wording.** "The Zheng variants are given the exact P (the paper's assumption). Our receivers use a fixed
  path cap of 8, which equals the true P of this channel family. Both sides therefore use knowledge of P."
- **What does not change.** The observations, the gains and the H5 PASS verdict: they compare receivers that both use P.
  Only the implied extra advantage of ours (not needing P) is withdrawn.
- **Scope consequence.** This applies to every result whose receivers use the cap, not only to this card:
  - P1 ([res-20261015-02](res-20261015-02.md));
  - the CRB tier and the genie-support audit;
  - E-1 and E-2, where the phantom harm is "one phantom displaces one true path in a fixed 8-slot budget".
  Robustness to a mismatched cap (cap ≠ true P) is untested.
