## Observation

The complete P1 grid comprises 104,000 receiver-input keys on 1,000 fresh physical frames, U/G, rho .3/.5, and 4.5–8.0 dB SNR. M changes the receiver cap at true P=8. T changes the generator to true P=6 or 10 at fixed cap 8 and compares B relative to the matched true-channel reference. N supplies a calibrated single-frame variance estimate to the receiver in G/rho .3. The frozen `B` receiver uses CODELAY after a nonzero F0 syndrome.

In M, the cap-12 shift versus cap 8 ranges from −0.087 to +0.106 dB. All four paired intervals are within ±0.25 dB. At cap 6, the four lower confidence bounds range from +1.685 to +2.712 dB. At fixed cap 8, true P=10 increases the B-minus-A3 gap with lower bounds of +1.282 to +1.637 dB across cells. Under estimated noise, the B crossing shifts by +1.189 dB [0.809, 1.579] at cap 8 and +0.992 dB [0.608, 1.488] at cap 12.

Of 24,000 eligible M keys, 7,462 triggered at both caps. On these keys, the CODELAY-minus-scalar rescue gap fell from 15.02 pp [14.19, 15.84] to 11.46 pp [10.68, 12.24]. The ratio was 0.763 [0.716, 0.811], with zero invalid draws. A2 retained a final co-delay pair on 7,457/7,462 cap-12 keys (99.93%). Both nominal caps were attained on 7,421 common-trigger keys, where the ratio was 0.765 [0.719, 0.813].
