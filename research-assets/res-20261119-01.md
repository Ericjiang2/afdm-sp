## Design (frozen)
- Controlled P1-based eight-path channel with a unique strongest path (background paths clipped to [.1, .4] of it) and a designated
  path at power ratio R ∈ {1.5, …, 128}.
- The designated path's delay is masked in every F0 selection step and unmasked at the first exchange ("forced miss"). A2 (scalar Γ)
  and CODELAY start from the same saved state.
- Frames 55000–55999, ρ ∈ {.3, .5}, 4.5 dB. The SNR was chosen by a leverage rule on a separate F0-only calibration block.
- **Primary measurement**: the paired preference Q_a = 1{designated phantom gain > designated missing-path gain} under one prespecified
  deletion slot. The anchor is truth-free (strongest estimated path).
- **Per-unit closed form**: R_A2* = (T² − z²E²)/(z²(u+λ)²) and ξ = R/R_A2*, from Astra's finite-spacing window.
- 24,000 units; 4.40 core-hours.

## Observation
- **A2 prefers the phantom almost everywhere.** Global first-edit phantom shares at R = 1.5 / 3 / 12 / 128:
  - ρ = .3: 99.6 / 99.9 / 90.5 / 50.2%;
  - ρ = .5: 89.1 / 100 / 100 / 100%.
- **CODELAY recovers the masked path at low R**: 999/1000 (ρ = .3) and 1000/1000 (ρ = .5) at R = 1.5.
- **Paired preference.** A2's 50% point lies at ξ₅₀ = .127 [.118, .136] pooled; .144 (ρ = .3), .176 (ρ = .5). That is a flip at R ≈ 1–1.3,
  against a closed-form prediction near R ≈ 6–8.
- **The registered theory curve** (calibration block) matches the observed pair-Q share at ρ = .3, R ≥ 12 (deviations ≤ .06). It misses by
  ≈ 1.0 at R ≤ 6.
- **Secondary FER on triggered units.**
  - At ρ = .3, the A2 − CODELAY difference rises from 1.9 pp (R = 1.5) to 53.5 pp (R = 12), then falls to ≈ 37 pp for R ≥ 64.
  - At ρ = .5, both arms fail every frame at this SNR, so the FER read is uninformative there.
- **Diagnostics.** The anchor never fell on the wrong delay (0/24,000). Third-candidate global wins: 4.6% (A2) and 13.6% (CODELAY).

## Post hoc audits (not pre-registered; the reading rule for audit 2 was fixed before computing)
1. **Inputs are consistent.** E, u and λ match the scoring's internal arrays exactly (20/20 units).
2. **The phantom-side model is exact for A2**: median error 0.4–1.0%, Spearman ≥ .995. The CODELAY phantom model misses (83–95%), as in
   res-20261118-06.
3. **The missing-path side is overestimated.** Measured/modelled reward is .27 / .35 / .45 / .85 at ρ = .3, R = 1.5 / 4 / 12 / 64, and .39
   at ρ = .5. The residual's projection on the missing-path column is ≈ .50–.64 of E|h_w| at low R.
   - The soft mean is calibrated on average: γ = .93–1.03.
   - Aliasing via the d = ±1 resonance: 0 units.
4. **Decomposition.** The shortfall comes from the support's data-error term Σ_p A_p(x − μ)h̃_p projected on the missing-path column:
   median −.30 to −.37 of E·h_w at R ≤ 12 for both ρ. At ρ = .5 it stays at −.31 even at R = 64. At ρ = .3, R = 64 it falls to −.01, where
   most masked-F0 frames decode. Gain-error, noise and other-path terms each contribute < 1%. Terms b1 + b2 account for 80% (ρ = .3)
   and 90% (ρ = .5) of the shortfall at R ≤ 4, so the pre-fixed reading rule gives **SUPPORTED**.
   - Closure needed one extra term (the support atoms' true-versus-fitted Doppler); with it, closure holds to 2.5e-15.

## Explanation (candidate; partly tested post hoc; confidence M)
- **Detector absorption.** Round-1 detection runs with the missing path unmodelled, so the soft-symbol error e = x − μ acquires a
  component aligned with that path's contribution. Seen through the retained strong paths, it cancels about one third of the missing
  path's projection. The Berkson premise of the window (e independent of the residual) fails exactly along the missing path.
- **Consequences.**
  - The closed-form window is an inner region of scalar failure. On failed frames, the scalar loses a missing path to a phantom
    down to R ≈ 1.2–1.5.
  - CODELAY removes the phantom competitor, so even the attenuated path wins (P3).
  - This also accounts for res-20261118-06's 78% phantom share outside W (see the correction below: absorption is only part of it).
- **Not established.**
  - A validated derivation of the absorption fraction (≈ .35 here). A partial, untested derivation was added on 2026-11-19; see
    the update below.

## Update 2026-11-19: Astra derivation of α (untested; explicit correction of scope)
[Astra output](../.trellis/tasks/11-18-ratio-sweep/research/astra-absorption-derivation-20261119.md),
[brief](../.trellis/tasks/11-18-ratio-sweep/research/astra-absorption-derivation-brief.md).
- **Round 1 as found.** The second masked F0 pass: P-BiG-AMP (20 iterations), Gaussian QPSK demapping, LDPC sum-product (≤ 50
  iterations). μ comes from the LDPC APP, clipped to ±20.
- **Model (derived under stated assumptions, confidence M).** The local linear response of the soft detector to the unmodelled path
  gives α̂ = (E/T)·(u/(Nτ))·C_w, where C_w is the overlap of the missing-path column with the retained-channel symbol perturbations.
  - The sign is correct.
  - α → 0 on correctly decoded frames, and α approaches a plateau on undecoded frames.
- **Check.** With C_w = 1 assumed and the decode state assigned by hand (decoded only at ρ .3, R ≥ 64):
  - the predictions match the ρ = .5 plateau for R = 4–32 within .03;
  - they miss the gradual ρ = .3 transition (overpredicting by up to .24 at R = 32) and the high-R ρ = .5 cells.

  The agreement is partly built in by these two choices, so this is a semi-quantitative mechanism, not a validated closed form.
- **Correction of scope.** Absorption alone does not account for the misplaced flip. With the measured α, the corrected factor
  (1 − α)² ≈ .42 at R = 1.5, whereas the measured reward ratio is .27 (ρ .3). The direct term is itself below 1 (median a = .862 at
  ρ .3, R = 1.5). Claude's rough check from AUDIT2 cell medians: a + b1 + b2 + other ≈ .49, which matches the measured projection .50;
  its square, ≈ .24, is near .27. Medians do not combine exactly, so this is approximate. So detector absorption is the largest single
  term, but the direct-term shortfall also matters. The statement "absorption explains the misplacement" is narrowed to "absorption
  is the largest contributor".
- **Astra's proposed prospective test** (ρ .5, R 1.5, 30 units with ξ < F, rejection at ≥ 20/30 phantom wins) is not run. On the
  existing numbers an α-only window is already expected to fail, so the test would carry little new information. A useful test
  needs a model for the direct term as well.
  - Its dependence on detector and SNR.
  - A prospective test of a corrected window.

## Implications for the narrative (proposal; Eric decides)
- **Sentence 2 (mechanism)** is strengthened empirically. The phantom reward model is exact, the scalar fails far beyond the window,
  and CODELAY is unaffected.
- The **"closed form predicts the flip location"** claim should not be made as stated. Either add an absorption term and test it
  prospectively, or present the window as a sufficient (inner) failure condition.
  - *Correction (2026-11-19, from two independent proof checks; see [divergence table](../.trellis/tasks/09-22-tf-production-gap-audit/research/proofs-20261119/review-divergence-20261119.md)):*
    "sufficient (inner) condition" is not a theorem. Under ideal projection (zero remaining projections, κ = D) the window is an
    exact pairwise characterisation. Without residual control it is neither sufficient nor necessary on real frames. That the
    scalar fails far beyond the window is an empirical finding of this card.
