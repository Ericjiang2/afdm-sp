## Audit (research-checkpoint 3; post hoc)
- **The S reversal is the scalar collapsing, not the exact Γ improving.**
  - R1's FER is flat in η (65 / 70 / 64%).
  - R0 is best at η = 4 (13%) and fails at η = 8 (66%), where **none** of its 400 runs converged within the frozen 100 iterations (100% at
    η = 2, 95% at η = 4). S-lo also converges 0% at η = 8.
  - R1 received no numerical regularisation: zero jitter events. The first-round inputs are identical across η.
  - So the η = 8 cell measures the iteration budget as much as the grid.
- **The tiny X effect follows from the kernel and could have been computed before the freeze.** |D_N(δ)| = 0.900, 0.974 and 0.994 at
  δ = .25, .125 and .0625. At the standard 0.25-bin grid the split reward is already close to its coincidence limit, so a finer grid can
  add little. The 10 pp threshold was a guess, not derived. This is a design error in the prediction; the frozen verdict stands.

## Explanation (untested; confidence M)
- **The prediction fails because both effects are already saturated at standard settings.**
  - Exchange: the phantom reward is near full strength at 0.25 bin. CODELAY's immunity holds down to 1/16 bin (≈ 2% at every
    spacing), which is a prospective confirmation of the coincidence invariance at finite spacing.
  - SBL at 6 dB: the exact-Γ failure is already near-maximal at η = 2 (R1 fails about 65% at every η), so the grid cannot amplify it.
    The η dependence of G_B is the scalar's grid fit (better at η = 4) and its convergence budget (fails at η = 8).
- **Cheapest test of the convergence reading**: rerun R0 and R1 at η = 8 with a longer EM budget on archived keys. Not run.
- **Effect on the paper**: none on the accepted claims. The grid-fineness extension is not supported and stays out of the manuscript,
  except possibly as one sentence: CODELAY's spacing invariance holds down to 1/16 bin.
