## Design (frozen)
- F0+CSTATE pipeline; arms F0, A2 (scalar) and CD (`CODELAY`).
- ρ ∈ {.15, .20, .25, .30, .40, .50}; scenarios U and G; SNR 3–12 dB plus 4.5, 5.5 and 6.5 dB.
- **Fresh frames 5400–5799** (400; 5000–5399 overlapped the archive and was skipped by rule). 62,400 keys; 6.49 core-hours.
- **Crossing target.** 1% FER. Censor-aware signed bounds; one shared physical-frame bootstrap with 20,000 draws.
- **Primaries.** Three contrasts, Bonferroni 98.33%:
  - P1: DiD of the CD-vs-F0 gain, .30 vs .50;
  - P2: CD's own change, .30 vs .50;
  - P3: best versus best.
- **Corrected before freeze.** The spectral principle gives no sign for the end-to-end gain (Astra), so the test is confirmatory of
  the archived direction. The direction below .30 is exploratory.

## Explanation (untested; confidence M)
- **System reading.** At a fixed total power, moving energy from the pilot to the data helps `CODELAY` down to about ρ = .25 at no cost.
  F0 and the scalar arm already lose there. So `CODELAY` lets the system run at ρ ≈ .25–.30 with about 5.0–5.2 dB required SNR at
  1% FER, about 1.1 dB below F0's best tested operating point.
- **Why the advantage grows** (post hoc readout added 2026-11-19; [mechanism readout](../.trellis/tasks/11-19-rho-sweep/research/mechanism-readout-20261119.md); confidence M).
  - **It is not "more F0 failures".** At equal SNR, F0 fails *less* at ρ = .30 than at .50: at 5 dB the trigger rate is .22 against
    .998 in U. The extra data power moves the LDPC waterfall down.
  - **What differs is per-trigger rescue.** At 5 dB, CD rescues .93 (U) and .91 (G) of the triggers at ρ = .30, against .02 and
    .005 at ρ = .50. A2 rescues only .33 and .29 at ρ = .30, because .89–.99 of its accepted first edits are phantoms (CD: .01–.07).
  - **Reading.** Lowering ρ opens an SNR band where F0 fails mainly on pilot-limited CSI (missed weak paths). That kind of failure
    is rescuable, and CODELAY converts it into crossing gain.
  - **Data-limited failures (updated 2026-11-19).** The claim that the ρ = .50 failures at 5 dB are data-limited no longer rests on
    inference alone. Independent October reference data (2,000 frames per cell) show that even the true-channel receiver fails
    1,886 and 1,883 of 2,000 frames there, so it rescues at most about 6% of F0 errors. At ρ = .30, true delays alone rescue at
    least about 83% (see the mechanism readout supplement). It remains post hoc, with different frames and receiver version.
- **Not claimed.**
  - That the window predicts the trend. It does not give a sign (Astra; the §0 correction).
