- **What it tests.** The one risk left untested in [res-20261119-03](res-20261119-03.md): whether Γ_U = Γ + u·Π_lo hurts genuine close
  co-delay pairs. P1 has none.

## Design (frozen)
- **Channel family.** MIXED8 ([res-20261118-02](res-20261118-02.md)): 8 rays on 6 delays, with an 8-slot receiver. Each frame has one
  0.35-bin doublet (Π_lo active, |D_N| ≈ .8) and one 0.75-bin doublet (Π_lo inactive, the within-frame control).
- **Operating point and frames.** ρ .5 at **7 dB**. The SNR was selected from F0/CD on old frames only (CD failed 54 / 4 / 1 of 96 at
  6 / 7 / 8 dB), and U was never run on those frames. Fresh frames 1000–1599 (600, independent).
- **Arms.** F0, A2, CD (closed-form `CODELAY`), U (Γ_U with per-trial projectors) and TRUE_GEOMETRY.
- **Primary measure.** Δ = FER(U) − FER(CD), with a margin of m = 1 pp: compatible if the upper bound ≤ m, conflict if the lower bound
  > 0, otherwise uncertain.

- **Secondary (frozen readouts):**
  - 0.35-bin doublets represented with two atoms: U 38.0% vs CD 42.2%, a difference of −4.17 pp [−5.83, −2.67].
    - Among the frames where the two arms' exchanges differed, 25 had a two-atom doublet under CD only and 0 under U only.
    - The omitted 0.35-bin energy fraction is +0.46 pp [0.30, 0.63] higher under U.
  - 0.75-bin doublets: identical (66.0% vs 66.0%), as expected with Π_lo inactive.
  - Singleton phantoms: 0 in both arms.
- **Post hoc (Claude, not pre-registered).** Of the 10 frames that fail only under U:
  - 4 have the 0.35-bin doublet as two atoms under CD and one under U, with U omitting 10–37% of that doublet's energy against ≤ 0.7%
    for CD;
  - 2 have two atoms in both arms but larger U omission;
  - 4 show no doublet difference.

## Explanation (untested; confidence M)
- **The H-conflict-C mechanism is present.** On genuine doublets spaced inside the Π_lo range, the extra difference-mode penalty u
  makes the exchange keep one atom where `CODELAY` keeps two. This happens in the direction predicted, and only on the active-spacing
  doublet.
- **The FER cost is small but not excluded.** The point estimate is +1.0 pp, and the post hoc split attributes roughly half of the
  U-only failures to the lost doublet.
- **Implication.** CSTATE never needed the floor: on P1, C-hi already equals CD. On channels with close doublets, the floor in CSTATE
  costs some resolution. The principle stands, but the single covariance for every stage does not come for free. The defensible
  operational reading is stage-specific placement:
  - an exact sum-mode penalty everywhere;
  - the difference-mode floor where the estimator is overcomplete (EM-SBL);
  - none needed in discrete exchange.

  This corresponds to branch **B2** of the skeleton drafts. Whether the SBL-side floor also costs doublet resolution in SBL has not
  been tested.
