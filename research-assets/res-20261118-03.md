## Interpretation
1. **Closed-form `CODELAY` (`u · D_N(Δν)` on co-delay pairs) is empirically lossless relative to `FULL` (`Γ(v)`).** Zero pass/fail
   flips occurred across `13,600` fresh keys (`1,976` triggers). Cross-delay symbol-variance fluctuations are unnecessary for end-to-end
   decoding; the geometry module requires only the scalar uncertainty sum `u = ∑v` rather than the symbol-variance vector `v`.
2. **Positioning of `CODELAY`: structural minimalism, not runtime speedup.** Skipping the `N = 1024` variance FFT reduces triggered
   `reopen_geometry` time by only `1.2%` (`1.44 ms`), because frequency-domain cross-correlation (`CPPField`) and local Doppler response
   evaluations dominate `reopen_geometry`. Do not claim a material runtime speedup for `CODELAY` over `FULL`.
3. **Triggered `F0+CSTATE` dominates Luo-style data-aided SBL in both reliability and latency on `P1`.** On identical frames, `CODELAY`
   achieves `1.8–5.3 dB` earlier `10^{-2}` FER at `ρ = 0.3` (`1.3–1.5 dB` at `ρ = 0.5`) with zero paired harms against both on-grid
   `L-hard` (`1403:0`) and off-grid-refined `diag-off` (`66:0`), at `1/12` to `1/16` of the single-core latency.

## Limits
- Formal joint `CODELAY` crossing gate is `inconclusive` because `U, ρ = 0.3` had `9.87%` simultaneously left-censored bootstrap draws at
  `5.0 dB` under independent censored-interval subtraction; with `400` frame clusters, `0` discordant clusters still leaves a `0.746%`
  one-sided 95% upper bound on cluster-level discordance.
- `L-hard`, `L-diag` and `diag-off` are ports sharing our two-stage screening/detector/LDPC harness (`res-20261017-03`,
  `res-20261017-04`), not Luo's in-EM Taylor update code; describe `L-hard` in the manuscript as a "Luo-style on-grid port".
- Single modulation/code/`N` (`QPSK`, `LDPC (3,6) n=2048`, `N=1024`), known `σ²`, path budget `8 = P` on `P1`.
