## Observation
- Universal clustered fractional-Doppler channels (4 taps; rays/tap 1+Poisson(lambda-1); nu = 2cos(theta)): the paper's
  production SP-AFDM receiver collapses (Jakes: FER .873 vs true-H .002 @9 dB); strongest existing parametric receiver
  leaves 2.89 dB (Jakes-4) / >3.48 dB (Jakes-8) @FER 1e-1; 16 slots (no stop rule) .58-.80 dB at 3.4x cost.
- True-H coded slopes: discrete 3.3, 5deg 3.7, 20deg 4.0, Jakes-4 6.6, Jakes-8 6.8, 16 separable paths 7.1:
  intra-tap Doppler spread is real diversity; sub-resolution clusters add ~none (group level).
- Effective-order prediction (per-tap Doppler-covariance eigenvalues with frame SNR > 1) 2.0/2.7/3.6 vs oracle 2/3/3-4.
- DPSS-BEM per tap with Slepian prior inside the SP joint estimator: within .36-.46 dB of true H on all five classes at
  .45x the parametric cost; loses .17 dB to the parametric receiver on discrete paths. All four pre-registered method
  gates failed (adaptivity claim dropped); worst-case uniformity is a post-hoc observation.

## Interpretation (scoped)
The regime that gives AFDM the most diversity — Doppler spread inside a delay tap — is the regime where fixed-order
parametric SP receivers fail; a band-limited per-tap representation recovers it. Primary contribution candidate:
problem + diversity/estimation-duality model. Method is a known class (Zemen DPSS-BEM; Benzine-Bemani 2025).

## Limits
One delay/PDP law, nu_max 2 bins, N 1024, rho .5, known sigma2, TF detector, 2 coded rounds; realized-power law in F1/F2/M1
vs expected law in F3/M2; hard P_eff count not proportional to coded slope.
