## Interpretation (scoped)
The practical iterative SP receiver supports 1.5-4x the load that this valid lower bound certifies, in every condition and
reference. A lower bound below practice cannot say how far the practical receiver is from the limit, in either direction.
Two looseness sources are structural: the learning cost charges the full Gaussian channel information (the receiver only
needs the channel to the accuracy the code tolerates), and the only channel information credited without the data is the
pilot's, while the practical receiver's later rounds learn the channel from decoded data (its R1 -> R10 cliff roughly
doubles, res-20261009-01). Answering the headroom question needs an upper bound on the supportable load or a lower bound
that credits data-aided channel information; neither is available from standard tools at this SNR.
