## Observation

In G at physical ρ=.3 and P=cap=8, the paired 1,000-frame cohort gave
FER=.01 crossings of 5.147, 6.384 and 6.790 dB for B_known, B_SF and B_DD.
R = B_SF − B_DD was −0.405 dB, with paired 95% CI [−0.724, +0.103].
A3_known, A3_SF and A3_DD crossed at 4.688, 4.880 and 6.064 dB, respectively.

The DD estimate reached its floor on 353/8,000 keys (4.41%). All floor hits
occurred on DD triggers at 4.5–5.5 dB. At 6.5 dB DD triggers averaged 0.638 times
the true variance and had zero floor hits. Round-1 delay sets agreed between
B_DD and B_known on all 8,000 paired observations.
