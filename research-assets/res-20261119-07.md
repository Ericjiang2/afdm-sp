## Design (frozen)
- **Rule G.** Floor a co-delay direction with u only when adding the mean-waveform data fails to halve its variance:
  2·pᴴV₁p > pᴴV₀p, with V₀ = (uD + λ*I)⁻¹ and V₁ = (uD + λ*I + BᴴB)⁻¹. Blocks larger than two use the positive eigenspace of 2V₁ − V₀
  on Dirichlet modes below 1. λ* depends on the stage: λ* = σ² in exchange and λ + u/N at SBL initialisation. Code: `gate.py`
  frozen at `bb5ad661`.
- **S leg (decisive).** E-3c SBL; arms R0, S-lo and G, where G is S-lo with Π_lo replaced by the gate subspace.
  - Settings: U/G × ρ .3 × 6–11 dB.
  - **Fresh frames** 5800–5999 ∪ 6200–6399: 400 frame clusters, 4,800 keys. 6000–6100 was skipped because E-3 calibration had used
    it.
- **X leg (transport).** MIXED8 at 7 dB, ρ .5; arms F0, CD and G-ex. G-ex applies the gate at every trial, at each local-pass entry
  and at the final refit. Fresh frames 1600–2199.
- **Primaries.**
  - S: Δ_S = FER(G) − FER(S-lo), frame-cluster 95% bootstrap, margin 1 pp. Upper bound < 1 → pass; lower bound > 1 → fail.
  - X: share of CD-resolved 0.35-bin doublets lost by G-ex, exact Clopper–Pearson at one-sided 97.5%. Upper bound < 5% → pass,
    with at least 74 resolved frames.
  - Joint: intersection–union; both legs must pass.

## Explanation (untested)
- **What the result supports (confidence H for the endpoints).** The stage-specific placement can be written as one rule: floor only
  what the data cannot identify, with the stage's ridge λ*.
  - In support exchange, λ* = σ² is small next to the data energy, so the rule is practically silent. It fired at 0.03% of trials
    and never changed an output, so it reproduces CD.
  - At SBL initialisation it floors 15 of S-lo's 18 modes on full blocks and costs at most 0.83 pp FER against S-lo.
- **Not supported.** That the gate picks *the right* modes. G is measurably worse than S-lo: the lower bound is above 0.
- **Where the 0.46 pp comes from (confidence L).** The candidate is the small-block modes that S-lo floors and the gate does not
  (median rank 0 against 1–2). H-dose's own secondary prediction failed on pair count: G outputs *fewer* co-delay pairs than S-lo,
  not more. Its convergence is slightly worse (1.5% vs 2.0%). The cheapest test is an arm "G plus S-lo on blocks of 24 atoms or
  fewer". It is not run.
- **Not claimed.** Optimality; transfer beyond these two settings; scalar R0 remains best in SBL by about 3.9 pp.
