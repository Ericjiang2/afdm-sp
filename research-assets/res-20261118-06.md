## Observation

Aggregate rescue and phantom shares were known at freeze. Per-unit outcomes and quantities were held back. Of the cap-8 triggered M-family keys, 7,667/8,106 (94.58%) passed the fixed checks. A2 three-class accuracy was 99.63%, up 5.31 pp over the same-subset always-phantom baseline (paired 95% CI [+4.47, +6.19] pp). Inside W, A2 made phantom-first edits on 5,654/5,654 keys and A1c on 157/5,654. Outside W, A2 did so on 465/596 keys.

For the admitted A2-winning phantom pairs, the modeled and measured Schur gains had Spearman .9991 and 0.80% median relative error in A2. The same pairs had Spearman −.0452 and 90.03% median relative error in A1c. Pooled values were .8688 and 6.99%.
