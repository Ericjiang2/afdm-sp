## Interpretation (scoped)
On the corrected generator, the CSTATE rescue on the production front end is not an F0-specific patch. It beats a stronger
front end at roughly half the time and stays 0.12–0.14 dB from genie CSI at rho .5. Its advantage grows at the lower pilot
fraction (rho .3), where the plain front end has an error floor. Global reopening matters: the local-only variant LOCAL
loses 0.18–1.34 dB. The formal H1 label is INCONCLUSIVE because of one under-powered high-SNR point. It is not
INCONCLUSIVE because of a weak or uncertain gain.
