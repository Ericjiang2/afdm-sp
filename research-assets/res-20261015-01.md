## Interpretation (scoped)
Under each reference the supportable load of an ideal receiver lies somewhere between ~.2-.65x and ~1.9-3.8x of the measured
practical cliff. Standard bounds cannot say whether the practical SP receiver has headroom. The gap between the two bounds has
one source: for MODEL and PHYS, per frame, UB - LB = Cst - A exactly (learning cost f and pilot term J are shared). So the whole width of the
bracket is the coherent term, Gaussian-input and fading-free on the upper side (~2.0-2.2 bit/symbol at 6.5 dB, i.e. QPSK's 2-bit cap or more, even before fading) and
the ZF-SIC QPSK bound on the lower side. The learning-cost accounting is not what keeps the question open. Tightening would
need a better upper bound on h(y) for a QPSK-driven output or a tighter coherent lower bound. Neither is a standard tool at
N = 1024. Under the protocol's route table (§5) this means the bound route stops here unless the user funds new theory.

  RESULTS.md is the screen's report-only score, not a bound.
