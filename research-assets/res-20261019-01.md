## Observation
- **Part A.**
  - All raw row files, configs and sources match their manifests; SHA-256 is unchanged before and after the run.
  - Key grids are complete: E-1 16379, E-2 R 32000, E-2 C 8000, E-3 9600, E-3b 2400.
  - The recount reproduces, to rel 1e-10:
    - 212 frozen scalars (means, contrasts, ratios and their CIs);
    - the success counts per arm;
    - the median NMSE.
  - Resampling by RNG frame instead of the frozen finer clusters changes no contrast's "CI excludes 0" status.
- **Part B.**
  - An independent CPP propagation, written from the specification without project code, matches the generator on 450
    frames (U, G and the cluster family C): maximum relative error 8.9e-13.
  - 140 parameter-law tests show no Holm rejection at α .01. Redraw rates match the historical 48.6% / 58.2%.
  - Determinism, the D1/D2/A1 invariants and the claimed frame sharing hold, including the EP/SP shared channel and
    noise.
  - Every comparison uses σ² = 10^(−SNR/10) relative to unit pilot + data energy. The E-3 family's internal scaling
    (data power .5, scale √1.4 at ρ .3) is a coordinate change: offset < 2e-15 dB.
  - Measured SNR is within .055 dB of nominal in all 29 groups.
  - One MINOR: family C has 1 + Poisson(3) rays per tap (mean 4), as its protocol already states.

## Conclusion (bounded)
The saved evidence behind the TCOM main line is internally consistent. Its channel generator, operator, power split
and SNR axis are as specified and shared across the compared arms. No accepted number changes.

## What this does not establish
- Correctness of the receivers' or decoders' logic: info-bit errors are trusted as stored, not recomputed from bits.
- Readouts outside the recount: E-1's doublet census, E-3b's |Δν| share, mean atoms, E-3c and the 10-18 cards.
- That the channel law is physically representative. Scope limits are unchanged: delay redraw, unit power, two Doppler
  laws.

common SNR definition). Not a performance claim.
