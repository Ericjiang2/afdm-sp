## Interpretation
1. **Missing co-delay uncertainty coupling, not nominated strong-ray off-grid leakage, drives the majority of scalar phantom edits.**
   Removing the anchor ray's orthogonal soft-mean residual leaves `233/240` scalar anchor-delay repeats intact (`U` median `26.71` vs
   `R` median `−0.027`), while restoring only the co-delay block flips `232/235` fixed pairs and recovers a missing true delay on
   `64.6%` (`87.1%` within one `0.25`-bin grid step) of repeated-delay units.
2. **Cross-delay symbol-variance fluctuations are inactive at step zero.** Constant-variance `C` (`v̄ · 1`) matches heterogeneous `F`
   across `100%` of raw and clean action classes (`|F - C|` median `~1e-4` vs `U` median `26.7`).
