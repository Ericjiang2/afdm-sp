## 1. Stripping Names: Operators, Symmetries, and Structural Couplings in `A1 + A3`

Let $\mathbf{A} = \mathbf{\Lambda}_{c_2}\mathbf{F}_N\mathbf{\Lambda}_{c_1} \in \mathbb{C}^{N\times N}$ be the discrete affine Fourier transform (DAFT), with transmitted time-domain block $\mathbf{s} = \mathbf{A}^H\mathbf{x}$ preceded by a chirp-periodic prefix (CPP) of length $L_{\mathrm{CPP}}$:
$$s[-n] = s[N-n] e^{-j2\pi c_1(N^2 - 2Nn)}, \qquad n = 1, \dots, L_{\mathrm{CPP}}.$$

1. **Physical & Timing-Reference Layer (`A1` Multi-User / Grant-Free / Asynchronous / Novel Multiple Access)**:
   - In multi-user uplink (`AFDMA`, `DAFT-s-AFDMA`, `Grant-Free MIMO-AFDM`, `AFDM-SCMA`, `AFDM-FTN`), $K$ geographically distributed users transmit toward an $N_{\mathrm{bs}}$-antenna receiver with distinct propagation delays / timing offsets $\tau_u \in [0, \tau_{\mathrm{async}}]$, carrier frequency / Doppler spreads $\nu_{u,p} \in [-\nu_{\max}, \nu_{\max}]$, multipath delays $\ell_{u,p} \in [0, \ell_{\max}]$, and spatial signatures $\mathbf{a}_{u,p} \in \mathbb{C}^{N_{\mathrm{bs}}}$.
   - **Fundamental symmetry breaking vs OFDMA**:
     - In OFDMA ($c_1 = 0$), any user timing offset $\tau_u$ satisfying $\tau_u + \ell_{\max} \le L_{\mathrm{CP}}$ acts diagonally in frequency ($\operatorname{diag}(e^{-j2\pi m \tau_u / N})$), preserving strict inter-user subcarrier orthogonality!
     - In AFDM ($c_1 \ne 0$), shifting a user's waveform by $\tau_u$ in time shifts its DAFT support by **`2 N c_1 \tau_u \pmod N` indices** AND induces a **chirp-periodic boundary phase jump** if $\tau_u + \ell_{u,p}$ crosses or mismatches the receiver's fixed de-CPP window! Consequently, quasi-synchronous or timing-asynchronous uplink users (even within $L_{\mathrm{CPP}}$, or across consecutive blocks when $\tau_u + \ell_{u,p} > L_{\mathrm{CPP}}$) suffer **catastrophic inter-user DAFT index collision** $(m + 2Nc_1(\tau_u + \ell_{u,p}) + \nu_{u,p}) \bmod N$ and piecewise phase twist that direct $\mathbf{F}_N \to \mathbf{A}$ OFDMA / synchronous AFDMA receivers fail to untangle.
2. **Spatial-Multiplexing & Full-Diversity Algebraic Layer (`A1` High-Rate `MIMO-AFDM` & Analytical `PEP` Bounds)**:
   - Single-antenna AFDM achieves full delay-Doppler diversity $P$ because non-overlapping affine shifts $(2Nc_1 \ell_p + \alpha_p) \bmod N$ ensure $\operatorname{rank}(\Delta \mathbf{X}_{\mathrm{eff}}) = P$ for any $\Delta \mathbf{x} \ne \mathbf{0}$.
   - In **spatial-multiplexing / multi-stream `MIMO-AFDM`** ($N_t$ transmit antennas, rate $R = N_t$ symbols per subcarrier, or pre-chirped / cyclic delay-Doppler shifted `CDDS-AFDM` / `ST-AFDM` / `AFDM-FTN`), naive spatial multiplexing or $c_2$-hopping loses transmit diversity or collides across antennas' affine footprints when $N_t P (2(k_{\max}+\kappa)+1) > N$ (1D modulo-$N$ aperture exhaustion!), whereas rate-1 `CDDS-AFDM` sacrifices spatial multiplexing rate by $1/N_t$. Designing structured algebraic precoding / constellation-chirp rotation with **closed-form pairwise error probability (`PEP`) and tight high-SNR `BER` bounds** directly targets this 1D affine aperture vs spatial-rate trade-off.
3. **Transform-Domain Inference & Online Parameter Adaptation Layer (`A3`)**:
   - Under dual-fractional dispersion, multi-antenna interference, faster-than-Nyquist (`AFDM-FTN`) colored noise/ISI, or non-stationary narrowband/chirp jamming (`Peng Yuan TWC'26`, `Zou TWC'26`), the effective DAFT Gram matrix loses narrow-band sparsity, or the optimal waveform parameters $(c_1, c_2, \text{pre-chirp / power / permutation})$ depend on the **instantaneous** per-frame channel/interference realization.
   - Where published AFDM detectors (`Huang & Sui TWC'26` `PD-DFE`, `Xu & Niu TWC'26` `Affine-BCJR`, `Shen & Yuan TWC'25` `TZP-AFDM`, `Dai & Luo TWC'26` `AFDM-FTN`, `Qu Luo TWC'25` / `Shen TVT'26` `MIMO-AFDM`) either hit a high-SNR truncation/error-propagation floor or incur $\mathcal{O}(N^3)$ / exponential complexity, a structured algorithm or online selector must clear `>= 10x` error reduction (floor-breaking) or `>= 10x` wall-clock speedup (compute ledger, across $N=64..512$ with fair matrix caching and `CoarseFine` / single-stage checks) while demonstrably failing or degenerating if $c_1 = 0$.
