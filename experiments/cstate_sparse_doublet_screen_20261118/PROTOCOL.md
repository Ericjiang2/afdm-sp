# Eight-ray sparse doublet CPU screen

Exploratory continuation of E-2. Its J4 arm averaged 16.07 rays against an eight-slot receiver. Here both families carry exactly eight rays. MIXED8 has delays 0, 6, 13, 22, 31, and 43, with equal pre-normalization amplitudes in the doublets at 6 and 22. Their separations are 0.35 and 0.75 Doppler bins, alternating assignment by frame. The superposition evolves within each frame. SINGLE8 moves the two added rays to delays 50 and 60 and keeps their Dopplers and pre-normalization gains. Each channel is separately normalized to unit physical operator energy per sample. Message, pilot, and noise draw are paired by frame. Doppler centers and complex phases are drawn afresh per frame. Physical pilot fraction is 0.5.

Reuse the E-2 frame assembly, F0, trigger, exchange, fine search, scalar-joint detector and decoder. Run FULL, CODELAY (full scorer with constant variance at the original mean), A2, A2g and A2n; all share the same captured F0 state. The E-2 guard threshold is unaltered. Every continuation is triggered by the native nonzero syndrome. Include F0 pass-through in unconditional FER. Use existing headroom TRUE_GEOMETRY (gain estimation from observation) and TRUE_CHANNEL (fixed true gains) on every frame.

Calibrate only from F0 and the two references on twelve MIXED8 frames (0–11), at 8, 12, 16 and 20 dB. Select the smallest point where each reference has at most one failure and F0 has at most three. If references qualify but F0 does not, use the qualifying point with least F0 failures, ties by ascending SNR, and report the missed calibration target. With no qualifying reference, stop. At the selected point run 96 distinct evaluation frames (100–195) per family. CPU only, one thread, six-minute wall cap. No expansion or tuning based on evaluation outputs.

Read unconditional FER and exact binomial intervals per family, paired rescues and harms, extra atoms on true singleton delays (including close pairs), and doublet operator energy outside the final nominal Doppler span at that true delay. The projection uses true waveforms and final path coordinates with oracle least-squares coefficients, so it isolates representational omission. This is separate from the actual gain error and does not by itself imply decoder failure.

For a positive screen, both references and both covariance arms should have MIXED8 FER at most .05, each covariance arm should have fewer MIXED8 failures than A2/A2g/A2n, and each should have fewer SINGLE8 singleton phantoms than A2 and lower MIXED8 omitted doublet energy than both guards. Alternatives include a guard that preserves useful recovery, and good oracle FER with high method FER (acquisition or gain headroom). This is a screening criterion. With 96 frames one failure is .0104, and a zero-failure arm has a two-sided exact upper limit above .01.
