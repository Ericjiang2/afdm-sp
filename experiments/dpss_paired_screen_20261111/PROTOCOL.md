On continued failure it fits each remaining candidate single-tap replacement with an eight-column block centered at zero, chooses the largest penalized residual reduction, and repeats the matched terminal restart. Primary engineering screen: report the UNIFIED-minus-B FER .1 crossing in U and G, and UNIFIED-minus-WIDE in J4. Carry forward the proposed +.2 dB reference threshold and require all three contrasts to support it before considering a larger test. Report paired frame bootstrap intervals and raw discordance counts. Treat unbracketed crossings and unstable resamples as unresolved. No extrapolation. True-H curves show remaining headroom. Counts of actual widening calls show the computation cost; worker times are instrumented.

Competing outcomes: an adequate schedule stays within .2 dB in all three contrasts; a residual P1 gap despite widening suggests the schedule cannot replace B; a J4 gap despite widening suggests that late expansion on already selected delays is insufficient. Prior belief that all three point estimates are within .2 dB: .25. The strongest confound is narrowing the initial model enough to distort APPs used to select and refit every rescue candidate; results test this complete policy and do not attribute a gap to one stage. Fractional delay, a corrected physical-prior receiver, and the end-to-end scalar ablation are outside this bounded exploratory screen.
