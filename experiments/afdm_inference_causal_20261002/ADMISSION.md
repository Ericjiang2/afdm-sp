# Candidate admission and dependent experiment closure

The approved PLAN section 4 permits a documented no-go when the approximation or message prerequisite fails. This run exercised that branch after one mechanism-directed revision; it does not declare the whole research direction exhausted.

## CORE-01: not admitted

The candidate is one global affine observation surrogate with the bilinear residual neglected, followed by full Gaussian/discrete inference with a legitimate prior/site decomposition. Exact affine Schur means, all covariance blocks, support-degenerate limits, Gaussian factor deletion and partial Jacobians are validated in gaussian_blocks.py and four targeted tests. The complete frozen-surrogate EP equations and their limits are in the task research/joint-message-theory.md. No production detector replacement was implemented or qualified.

The exact-posterior-mean tangent passes its frozen remainder screen in only 1/24 pilot-present conditions. Revision 1 replaces its center/slope by the minimum-MSE real augmented affine projection under the SAME exact posterior. It still passes only 1/24 pilot-present conditions. All positive covariance modes are retained, so this result cannot be attributed to discarded numerical support. The unmodeled best-affine residual is on average 0.339428 times the noise energy. This is a test of the prespecified 0.1-noise engineering screen, not a universal impossibility theorem or a FER bound. See REMAINDER.md, SLR.md and research/candidate-admission.md for proof, raw paths and precise restrictions.

Additional diagnostics do not establish the initially hypothesized practical root cause: CE initialization differences largely attenuate; actual directional mismatch has uncontrolled terminal-stage confounding; small-model joint output is not uniformly overconfident. Existing scalar P-BiG-AMP already includes the variance-product term. A matrix replacement would therefore lack both validated model scope and an identified causal benefit.

## Boundaries

Residual-aware nonlinear inference, mode-conditioned approximations and efficient direct marginal-likelihood approaches remain untested spaces. Their viability or novelty is not established here. The mathematical oracle covers N=2/3 with six exogenous clusters; the archived diagnosis covers 256 exposed N=1024 baseline frames with six failures. Neither licenses a blanket production no-go. This file records the negative branch of this finite task, not a smaller replacement success criterion.

The exact remainder was not computed for the six N=1024 production-cohort failures: their true joint posterior is unavailable. Difficult small-model conditions are not substitutes for those frames. Thus failure-frame local Gaussian validity remains unverified, not falsified by this run. This limitation prevents a production-wide rejection or acceptance claim.
