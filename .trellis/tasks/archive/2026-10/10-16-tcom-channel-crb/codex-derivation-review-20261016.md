1. **OK — Operator and derivatives (§§1.1–2).** The CPP phase, positive Doppler sign, cyclic shift, and unitary DAFT agree with the code. Since Doppler multiplies the **output** sample \(k\), \(\partial_{\nu_i}T=g_iDT_i\) is correct. **Fix:** explicitly construct \(B=[B'_{\rm reg}\operatorname{diag}(g),B_{\rm reg},jB_{\rm reg}]\): `regressors(derivative=True)` returns **unit-gain** Doppler derivatives and does not multiply by \(g_i\).

2. **OK — Analytic traces (§2.1).** All three formulas are correct for integer delays in \(0,\ldots,N-1\). Equal delays give identical CPP phases, which cancel pointwise, including with the diagonal \(D\) weights. Different delays produce a nonidentity cyclic shift with zero diagonal, hence exactly zero trace regardless of Doppler or CPP phase. **Fix:** retain the delay-domain assumption and evaluate removable Dirichlet-kernel singularities stably, especially at \(\Delta=0\).

3. **OK — FIM constants and orderings (§2.2), subject to regularity.** With \(\sigma^2=E|w_k|^2\), DA and MCRB correctly have \(2/\sigma^2\); the proper-complex Slepian–Bangs covariance term correctly has coefficient **one**, without an additional two. MCRB retains all path/parameter cross-terms; only pilot–data terms vanish. The missing-information identity gives \(J_{\rm true}\preceq E_dJ_{\rm DA}=J_M\), and inverse convexity gives \(E_d[J_{\rm DA}^{-1}]\succeq J_M^{-1}\). **Fix:** state parameter-independent data law, positive-definiteness and expectation conditions; verify \(E[d]=0\) and \(E[dd^H]=(1-\rho)I\) for the **actual coded** data, rather than merely uncoded QPSK. The complex-Gaussian convention agrees with [Delmas et al.](https://www-public.imtbs-tsp.eu/~delmas/articlesPDF.JPD/Elsevier/45_2015.pdf).

4. **MAJOR — PI-CRB interpretation (§2.2).** Its formula is correct for an artificial Gaussian family whose covariance is frozen at the evaluation point. “Not a lower bound for all estimators” is accurate for the actual channel experiment, but calling it a matched bound for all “pilot-only” estimators is unsupported: using only a mean-based objective does not change the observation law. **Fix:** call PI an auxiliary reference without a general bound guarantee for F0. GD is exact under the Gaussian-data model; no general ordering with the QPSK CRB follows.

5. **BLOCKER — Known gain normalization changes the relevant CRB (§§1.2–2.3).** The physical parameter space satisfies \(c(\theta)=\sum_i|g_i|^2-1=0\). An estimator locally unbiased **along this manifold** need not satisfy ambient \(3P\)-dimensional unbiasedness, so the unconstrained bound does not cover every locally unbiased estimator of the physical model. **Fix:** either explicitly restrict claims to ambient-model unbiasedness, or use a tangent basis \(U\) with \(\nabla c\,U=0\) and covariance bound \(U(U^TJU)^{-1}U^T\). For white input and full tangent rank, the channel-bound numerator becomes \((3P-1)\sigma^2/2\).

6. **MAJOR — H-level expression is correct, but its justification needs revision (§2.3).** For an estimator locally unbiased for \(h(\theta)=\operatorname{vec}H(\theta)\), the functional CRB gives the **exact** bound \(\operatorname{tr}(J^{-1}\Re K)\); no delta approximation is needed. Local unbiasedness of \(\hat\theta\) alone does not establish exact unbiasedness of nonlinear \(H(\hat\theta)\). The identity \(3P\sigma^2/(2\|H\|_F^2)\) is correct only when \(\Re K\) has full rank. **Fix:** state functional unbiasedness explicitly and specify singular-case handling. Coincident paths or zero gains can make parameter information singular while some channel functions remain estimable; a blind pseudoinverse substitution is insufficient.

7. **MAJOR — Van Trees rejection is too categorical (§1.2).** The ordinary ambient-density formula is unsuitable as written: arcsine endpoints are nonregular, folded-normal density does not vanish at zero, and normalized gains lack an ambient Lebesgue density. But “BCRB is zero or undefined” overstates this: infinite prior information does not justify applying a theorem whose boundary conditions fail, and the actual U generator also clips Dopplers and conditions draws through delay acceptance. **Fix:** say “the standard unweighted Van Trees formula is not applicable in this parameterization.” Keep constrained conditional MCRB for the intended local reference; for an all-estimator Bayesian benchmark, consider finite-difference [Weiss–Weinstein bounds](https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.07037), formulated for the actual prior and channel loss.

8. **OK — Genie delays preserve a lower bound, with explicit unbiasedness scope (§1.3).** An estimator ignoring the revealed delays remains available in the genie experiment. Thus a valid fixed-delay functional CRB bounds its risk when the required local unbiasedness holds for that fixed-delay submodel; discreteness does not invalidate this argument. **Fix:** state this directly. Also distinguish DA averaging: \(E_d[J_{\rm DA}^{-1}]\) requires unbiasedness conditional on each revealed data realization, which does not follow from unbiasedness after averaging over data.

9. **BLOCKER — The §5 BELOW/efficiency interpretation is statistically invalid.** CRBs constrain expected squared error, not individual errors or their median logarithmic ratios. Even an exactly efficient unbiased scalar Gaussian estimator has median squared-error/CRB about \(0.455<1\), so BELOW cannot establish bias or prior exploitation. Conditioning on successful decoding also changes the experiment and removes the unconditional CRB guarantee. **Fix:** retain median ratios and success-only results as descriptive statistics; remove causal interpretations of BELOW. Base bound comparisons on unconditional arithmetic mean NMSE versus mean bound, with paired frame-bootstrap uncertainty.

10. **MAJOR — Gates provide good factor-two coverage but need independent anchors (§6).** G3(a,c), if implemented independently, should detect a factor-two error; G4 can pass when its two sides share the same mistake, and G5 depends on conditioning and finite-SNR bias. **Fix:** add an exact known-Doppler, single-complex-gain LS check, \(E|\hat g-g|^2=\sigma^2/\|b\|^2\); check real/imaginary noise variances \(\sigma^2/2\), score means, full covariance blocks, finite-difference step stability, rank/conditioning, and a covariance-only GD case. Define the MC standard-error calculation. In G2 build dense \(H\) **columnwise**: `Channel.apply` does not support a matrix input.
