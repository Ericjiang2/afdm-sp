## Q2. Off-grid / gridless super-resolution; Taylor/derivative atoms; confluent conditioning

1. Ekanadham, Tranchina, Simoncelli. 2011. IEEE TSP 59(10):4735-4744. "Recovery of sparse translation-invariant signals with continuous basis pursuit". https://www.cns.nyu.edu/pub/eero/ekanadham11-reprint.pdf [SN] — rel: continuous shifts via auxiliary interpolation (Taylor-type) atoms; convex in amplitudes, nonconvex in shift. PDF binary, unread. Origin of derivative-atom idea.
2. Knudson, Yates, Huk, Pillow. 2014. NIPS 27:1215-1223. "Inferring sparse representations of continuous signals with continuous orthogonal matching pursuit". https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2014/hash/9825c157f1333c2bba2747f1c6500b8c-Abstract.html [SN] — rel: greedy COMP, Fourier-domain shift refinement, beats CBP on runtime/accuracy. NOTE: a search summary wrongly credited COMP to Ekanadham et al.; correct authors here.
3. Moitra. 2015. STOC [SN venue]. arXiv 1408.1681 "Super-resolution, Extremal Functions and the Condition Number of Vandermonde Matrices". https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.1681 [AB] — rel: sharp threshold: stable iff cutoff m > 1/Delta+1; below (1-eps)/Delta impossible; cond-number phase transition; first matrix-pencil noise bounds. Simple nodes only.
4. Li, Liao. 2021. ACHA 51. arXiv 1709.03146 "Stable super-resolution limit and smallest singular value of restricted Fourier matrices". https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.03146 [AB] — rel: clump model; SRF=(M Delta)^-1; non-asymptotic sigma_min lower bound = weighted l2 sum over clumps; MUSIC degradation exponent = largest clump size. Simple nodes only.
5. Batenkov, Demanet, Goldman, Yomdin. 2020. SIMAX 44(1) [SN venue]. arXiv 1809.00658 "Conditioning of partial nonuniform Fourier matrices with clustered nodes". https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.00658 [AB] — rel: sharp sigma_min lower bound, polynomial in 1/SRF, exponent = max cluster cardinality. Simple nodes, no derivatives.
6. Batenkov, Diab. 2023. ACHA 65:181-208 [SN venue]. arXiv 2203.11923 "Super-resolution of generalized spikes and spectra of confluent Vandermonde matrices". https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.11923 [AB] — rel: MOST RELEVANT MATH. Spikes + derivatives = confluent nodes. Sharp asymptotic sigma_min bounds for rectangular confluent Vandermonde, partial clustering; min-max error for grid nodes. Explicit exponents NOT extracted.
7. Batenkov, Goldman, Yomdin. 2021. Inf. Inference 10(2) [SN venue]. arXiv 1904.09186 "Super-resolution of near-colliding point sources". https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.09186 [AB] — rel: p-node cluster, SRF=(Omega Delta)^-1; stable iff eps <~ SRF^(-2p+1); cluster-node minimax error ~ SRF^(2p-1) eps; Matrix Pencil attains. Non-derivative cluster nodes.
8. Wei, Yuan, Li, Yuan, Ng. 2022. IEEE TWC [SN venue]. arXiv 2101.05629 "Off-grid Channel Estimation with Sparse Bayesian Learning for OTFS Systems". https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.05629 [AB] — rel: OTFS off-grid offsets as SBL hyperparams (EM); estimates original not effective DD channel; decoupled 2D variant. Taylor linearization NOT in abstract (Taylor claim from snippet [SN]). Related fractional-Doppler SBL with linearization-error critique: Zhang et al. ## Verdict: PARTIALLY published. Method class: yes. SP-AFDM + conditioning + error-floor combo: not found.

- Method class published: 2504.13762 [FT-x] (Def. 4 confirmed twice): sub-paths sharing integer delay and Doppler, differing in fractional Doppler; DPSS-BEM per grid point; pilot CS; waveform-agnostic; AFDM compared. Covers "same delay bin, Doppler separation < 1/T" with a DPSS atom set. Centroid + Slepian atoms already present in grid-point form. Any novelty claim must differentiate explicitly against it.
- AFDM closed-form estimation-error -> BER bound: published (2504.18901 [FT-x]); not for same-delay clusters, not SP.
- SP + BEM: OTFS only (2309.08177 [FT-x]); no fractional-Doppler or same-delay handling. AFDM SP found = sparse OMP (ICCT 2024 [SN]), no BEM.
- Confluent / clustered conditioning: published in math (1408.1681, 2203.11923, 1904.09186, 1809.00658, 1709.03146). No application to OTFS/AFDM CE found.
- Diversity vs separable-path count: qualitative only (2311.15595 [AB]; Surabhi [AB]). No unresolved-cluster PEP analysis found.
- Not found: a paper combining (a) same-delay Doppler clusters below 1/T, (b) centroid + confluent-derivative (Taylor/Slepian) atoms, (c) SP turbo receiver in AFDM, (d) sigma_min/cond of cluster dictionary vs Doppler separation delta, (e) error-floor link.
- Unverified claim "doublet cond ~ delta^-2 vs bounded confluent basis": no opened source gives that exponent. 1904.09186 gives SRF^(2p-1) minimax noise amplification for p-node clusters (noise amplification, not cond number). 1809.00658 gives sigma_min polynomial in 1/SRF, exponent = max cluster size. Derive sigma_min of [centroid, derivative] atom pair vs delta on the DD grid before asserting delta^-2. Moitra 1408.1681 implies sub-Rayleigh recovery needs observation span ~1/Delta: relevant to pilot-count / T trade-off.
