1. **[derived] Yes. The gate means strictly less than one bit of mutual information in a frozen Gaussian working model.** On the isolated co-delay block used by the gate, fix the mean-waveform dictionary \(B\), uncertainty penalty \(\Gamma=uD\succeq0\), ridge \(\lambda_*>0\), and contrast \(p\ne0\). Set \(V_0=(\Gamma+\lambda_*I)^{-1}\) and \(V_1=(V_0^{-1}+B^HB)^{-1}\). For any \(c>0\), take \(h\sim\mathcal{CN}(0,cV_0)\) and \(y=Bh+n\), with independent proper complex Gaussian noise \(n\sim\mathcal{CN}(0,cI)\). Gaussian conditioning gives \(\operatorname{Cov}(h\mid y)=cV_1\). Unit-noise normalization gives \(V_0,V_1\) themselves. In receiver units, \(c=\lambda_*=\sigma^2\) in support exchange and \(c=\lambda_*=\beta_0^{-1}=\lambda+u/N\) at sparse Bayesian learning (SBL) initialization. In the latter, \(cV_0=(I+\beta_0\Gamma)^{-1}\) is the **effective** prior covariance after incorporating \(\Gamma\) into the unit-variance gain prior.  
   **[derived]** A proper complex scalar of variance \(v\) has entropy \(\log_2(\pi e v)\). Subtracting conditional entropy gives \(I(p^Hh;y)=\log_2[(p^HV_0p)/(p^HV_1p)]\), so the gate fires exactly when \(I<1\). Equality leaves the direction unfloored. This is information averaged over \(y\) at the frozen design, with the other gains marginalized. It does not measure information delivered by the particular received sample or by the physical channel with data-dependent soft decisions.

2. **[derived] The evidence has a related determinant term. Its per-mode value equals the gate information only in the appropriate independent modes.** At fixed gain-prior precision \(P\succ0\) and noise precision \(\beta\), write \(K_0=P+\beta\Gamma\) and \(K_1=K_0+\beta B^HB\). The Gaussian log-determinant cost in bits decomposes as
   \[
   \log_2\frac{\det K_1}{\det P}
   =\log_2\frac{\det K_0}{\det P}+\underbrace{\log_2\frac{\det K_1}{\det K_0}}_{I(h;y)\text{ under the effective prior}} .
   \]
   **[derived]** In coordinates that whiten the effective prior and diagonalize \(K_0^{-1/2}\beta B^HBK_0^{-1/2}\), each eigenvalue \(s\) contributes \(\log_2(1+s)\) bits of waveform information. An arbitrary contrast mixes these modes. Its marginal variance ratio generally differs from an individual determinant contribution.  
   **[derived]** At exact duplication with \(P=\alpha I\), the difference mode has \(Bp=\Gamma p=0\). A floor \(rpp^H\) adds \(\log_2(1+\beta r/\alpha)\) to the first determinant term while its waveform information stays **zero**. Thus the exact block really does remove a determinant cost in this limit. The observed reversal in frame-error rate does **not** follow. Type-II learning also changes the fit, gain variances, noise precision and pruning. **[conjecture, low confidence]** The missing determinant cost mediates the reversal. The prospective intervention established that flooring low modes recovered 76.3% of the gap, without isolating that mediator.

3. **[derived] The one-bit reading is a relabelling. It adds no frame-error sign, block-size attribution or signal-to-noise prediction.** At a threshold of \(t\) bits, a fixed contrast is floored when \(2^t p^HV_1p>p^HV_0p\). Raising \(t\) can only add qualifying fixed contrasts in either stage. For larger blocks the implemented positive-eigenspace rank on eligible Dirichlet modes cannot decrease, although its eigenvectors can rotate. These statements hold at a common receiver state and imply no ordering of downstream errors.  
   **[derived]** The paired test found an excess of \(22/4800=0.458\) percentage points against S-lo, the fixed low-mode floor. Of those 22 net excess failures, 16 occurred at 6 dB, a post hoc observation. Exchange lost 0 of 116 baseline-resolved doublets at the tested threshold. **[conjecture, low confidence]** Omitted small-block floors caused the excess. The rank census motivates that hypothesis, but neither the census nor the bit identity assigns failures to those blocks.

4. **[derived] The scalar phantom has an exact quadratic coding-cost interpretation.** At coincidence the waveform sees only the aggregate \(H=h_j+h_c\). Holding \(H\) fixed, the scalar uncertainty cost falls from \(u|H|^2\) to \(u(|H/2|^2+|H/2|^2)=u|H|^2/2\). At noise precision \(\beta\), the equal split therefore saves \(\beta u|H|^2/(2\ln2)\) bits in the quadratic part of a Gaussian negative log density. The exact block charges \(u|H|^2\) in both representations. The coordinate ridge separately saves \(\beta\lambda|H|^2/(2\ln2)\) under either representation.  
   **[analogy]** Calling the phantom a *shorter description of the same waveform* captures that quadratic saving. A complete description-length comparison also needs normalization, a support code and a precision convention for continuous gains. The saving concerns the sum mode and depends on \(|H|^2\). The one-bit gate concerns information about the contrast.
