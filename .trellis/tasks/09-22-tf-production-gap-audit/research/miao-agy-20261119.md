## 1. Concrete Bar for "妙" (vs. Solid-but-Ordinary IEEE TCOM)
A **solid** TCOM paper spots a real mismatch (`uI` vs. `uD`), proves exactness/complexity of the fix, and reports disciplined gains with honest caveats. A genuinely **"妙"** paper clears four simultaneous bars:
1. **Compression without patches:** One concise structural law generates ≥2 seemingly separate phenomena with zero ad-hoc switches—replacing a list of exceptions with a single mechanism.
2. **Non-obvious reversal:** A strong communications researcher's first instinct gets the sign, boundary, or algorithm preference backwards before seeing the law.
3. **Out-of-sample risky prediction:** It makes a sharp, falsifiable prediction in a regime *not* used to construct it—one that could cleanly fail, not a mathematical relabelling of what was already observed.
4. **Actionable payoff:** It either unlocks a system operating regime baselines cannot reach, or yields a portable design rule that changes how receivers are built beyond this shell.

## 3. Three Ideas to Dig Out of the Existing Base

### Idea 1: Two-Barrier Pilot-Economy Phase Law (Virtual-Pilot $(u/N, d)$ Collapse + `max_edits` Wall)
- **One-sentence principle:** Once `CODELAY` removes co-delay phantoms, soft-decoded data ($\|\bar x\|^2 \approx N - u$) act as virtual pilots whose reacquisition depends on $\rho$ almost solely through $(u/N, d=\text{SNR}+10\log_{10}(1-\rho))$ and the combinatorial budget $K_{\rm edits} \ge k_{\rm miss}(\rho)$, so SP-AFDM's operating window is bounded above by the LDPC data-SNR cliff (`R-D`: genie `A3` fails 99% at $\rho=.5, 5$ dB) and below by edit starvation before the physical bootstrap cliff.
- **Risky prediction (outside build regime):** The $\rho=.15$ failure (`12+ dB`, and the `.91–.98` vs. `.77–.85` over-prediction by the $(u/N,d)$ table) is driven by `max_edits=2` starving when F0 misses $\ge 3$ paths (`93%` of $\rho=.15$ CD failures still miss $\ge 1$ true path after both edits while $u/N \le 0.31$). Raising `max_edits` $2 \to 6$ (early-stop on no gain; a $\rho=.3$ pilot saw *zero* benefit) at $\rho \in \{.10, .15, .20\}$ on fresh frames will: (i) pull $\rho=.15$ rescue back onto the $(u/N, d)$ surface, (ii) drop `CODELAY`'s 1% FER crossing by $\ge 0.8$ dB at $\rho=.20$ and $\ge 2.5$ dB at $\rho=.15$—pushing $\rho^* < 0.25$ and total required SNR *below* 5.0 dB—while barely helping scalar `A2` (which burns extra edits on phantoms), and (iii) expose the true physical bootstrap wall only at lower $\rho$ where $u/N$ jumps across the $(u/N,d)$ frontier.
- **Cheapest decisive check:** (a) Zero-cost stratification of saved `rho_sweep` $\rho=.15$ rows by `n_edits==2 & n_missed_after>=1`; (b) **~3–4 core-hours** fresh-frame run of `max_edits ∈ {2, 4, 6}` for `CD`/`A2` at $\rho \in \{.10, .15, .20, .25\}$, U/G, 3.5–10 dB (62,400 keys took 6.49 core-hours).
- **Kill criterion:** Killed if `max_edits=6` leaves $\rho=.15$ $>5$ pp below the $(u/N, d)$ table at 5–8 dB, OR yields $<0.4$ dB crossing gain at $\rho=.20$ and $<1.5$ dB at $\rho=.15$ (proving genuine APP-basin collapse rather than edit starvation).
- **New or relabelling?** **Genuinely NEW**—directly contradicts v1.4.2's "2 edits saturate / collapses below $\rho=.25$" claim and can improve Paper 1's headline system curve.

### Idea 2: Non-Berkson Self-Feedback (Stein–Onsager Absorption) Law of Undecoded Turbo CE
- **One-sentence principle:** Before LDPC codeword lock imposes discrete rigidity ($\partial\mu/\partial y \to 0$), feeding APP soft symbols $\mu_{\rm APP}(y)$ back into channel re-estimation leaves $\hat H\mu$ linearly responsive to $y$ ($\propto u/(N\tau)$), simultaneously absorbing thermal noise *below* $\sigma^2$ (`R-F`: `r_norm=0.807` on 2,413 undecoded Round-0 triggers vs. `1.094` decoded, killing DD $\hat\sigma^2$) and cancelling ~35% of missing paths (`b2≈-0.35`, shifting scalar `A2`'s flip 8× beyond Prop 2(d)'s Berkson window).
- **Risky prediction (outside build regime):** Replacing `raw_app` with LDPC `raw_check_extrinsic` (cavity decoupling: removing direct $\text{LLR}_{\rm det}(y)$ self-feedback) on identical Round-0 F0 states will *simultaneously* (i) raise undecoded `r_norm` from `0.807` to $\ge 0.95$ (rescuing DD $\hat\sigma^2$ without knowing $\sigma^2$), and (ii) cut missing-path absorption $|b_2|$ by $\ge 50\%$ in `ratio_sweep` ($R \in \{1.5, 4, 12\}$). *(Genuine risk: AFDM's dense DAFT mixes all $N$ symbols, so parity-check extrinsics may still absorb globally.)*
- **Cheapest decisive check:** **~1–2 core-hours** paired replay comparing `raw_check_extrinsic` vs. `raw_app` on 400 `sigma2_dd` frames (4.5–6 dB) and 300 `ratio_sweep` units ($R=1.5, 4, 12$).
- **Kill criterion:** Local-APP self-feedback is killed if extrinsic leaves undecoded `r_norm < 0.90` OR $|b_2| > 0.22$ (proving absorption is global dense-equalizer overfitting, not APP self-feedback—still a clean negative boundary).
- **New or relabelling?** **Genuinely NEW**—unifies three isolated negative/caveat cards (`res-18-05`, `res-19-01`, Prop 2(d) gap) into a causal turbo-CE test that could also solve Paper 1's known-$\sigma^2$ vulnerability.

### Idea 3: Oversampling-Ratio ($\eta$) Estimator–Geometry Duality (Replacing the 1-Bit Gate in EM-SBL)
- **One-sentence principle:** On an $\eta=1/\Delta\nu_{\rm grid}$ oversampled grid, $uD_\ell$'s Slepian spectrum splits into $1/\eta$ sum modes at $\approx \eta u$ and $1-1/\eta$ zero modes: fixed-$P$ continuous-Doppler search needs exact upper modes for split invariance (one-sided), whereas on-grid overcomplete EM-SBL needs a flat band around $u$ (two-sided) because $\approx 0$ modes break ARD (`R1`: 19%) while $\approx \eta u$ modes inflate $\mu^H\Gamma\mu$ in `sbl_rx.py`, crushing $\beta$ (`4.83→3.14`) and convergence (`94.8%→3.8%`, `R-B1`) and over-shrinking multi-atom off-grid clusters (`KM'` fixes `69/70`, `R-B2`).
- **Risky prediction (outside build regime):** (i) Holding `U2`'s E-step exact while capping $\Gamma$ *only* in the $\beta$-update (`quad` term) restores EM convergence from `<5%` to `>80%` and captures $\ge 70\%$ of `K'`'s FER rescue; (ii) coarsening the SBL grid from $\eta=4$ (`0.25` bin) to $\eta=2$ (`0.5` bin) cuts `U2−R0` $\beta$-deflation and FER gap by $\ge 50\%$, while $\eta=8$ (`0.125` bin) widens both.
- **Cheapest decisive check:** **~1–2 core-hours** on the 400-frame `sbl_residual_gap` split testing $\beta$-capped `U2` and $\eta \in \{2, 4\}$.
- **Kill criterion:** Killed if $\beta$-only capping keeps convergence `<20%` or recovers `<30%` of `K'`'s rescue, OR if `U2−R0` gap does not scale monotonically with $\eta$.
