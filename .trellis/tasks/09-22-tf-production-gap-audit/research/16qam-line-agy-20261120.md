## 1. Frozen AFDM (N=1024, 2Nc1=5): any risky, could-fail prediction?
- **No** [J]. In frozen integer-delay AFDM, the non-constant-modulus line yields no risky prediction of the interface-rank principle that could fail on real decisions:
  1. **Co-delay invariance at fixed (mu, u)**: Prop 1 (`theory-astra-v2.tex` eq. `af-full`, Rem. 3) [E] proves `(Gamma_F)_{ij}|_{d=0} = u D_N(dnu)` for *all* `v >= 0` (`rank S_AFDM = 1`) [D]. Redistributing `v` at fixed `(mu, u)` moves `Gamma_C` by identically zero [D], so Astra's fixed-`(mu, u)` check cannot fire on AFDM co-delay.
  2. **0-vs-1 scalar separation (`mu` alone vs `(mu, u)`)**: Algebraically, 16-QAM separates them—Gray bit-LLR posteriors at fixed `mu_n` span the full simplex interval `[v_min(mu_n), 1.8 E_s - |mu_n|^2]` (up to `9x` at `mu_n = 0`) [D]. Yet on `N=1024` uniform 16-QAM:
     - The QPSK clamp `sum max(E_s - |mu_n|^2, 0)` fails trivially (`+40%` to `+363%` error at 5-11 dB [N]: a coding bug, not a physics prediction).
     - The unbiased `mu`-only proxy `u_avg = max(N E_s - ||mu||^2, 0)` concentrates within `sqrt(0.32/N) = 1.8%` of `N E_s` [D], giving `1.6%-7.8%` median error in the failed-frame trigger regime `u/(N E_s) in [0.12, 0.36]` (`-16%` to `-18%` even under 25% unmodeled ISI) [N]. Moreover, any scalar `u_hat` preserves exact coincidence split-invariance `u_hat|h_1 + h_2|^2` in `CODELAY(u_hat)` [D]. Large relative error (`31%-103%`, occasionally hitting 0) appears only at `>=11-14 dB` where `u/(N E_s) <= 0.05` and `F0` already decodes [N].
  3. **Cross-delay `Gamma_F - Gamma_C`**: At `a1 = 2Nc1 = 5`, `|D_N(dnu + 5d)| <= 0.080` for all `|d| >= 2` on `|dnu| <= 6` (`<= 0.217` on physical pairs `|dnu| <= 4`; `0/24,000` aliasing in `res-20261119-01`) [E: `theory-astra-v2.tex`, `zero-cost-readouts-20261119.md` §1]. At 2-8 dB AWGN, 16-QAM has *lower* variance dispersion than QPSK (`CV 0.30-0.41` vs `0.77-3.83`; `|vhat[1]|/u = 0.007-0.011` vs `0.016-0.082`) [N]. Under 8-path AFDM fading, chirp diversity flattens per-symbol gain `g_n = ||H W e_n||^2` and doubly suppresses cross-delay (`max|Gamma_cross|/u ~ 1e-4`, `|h^H(Gamma_F - Gamma_C)h|/u <= 5e-4` at 8-12 dB) [D, N]. Thus `CODELAY = FULL` on 16-QAM AFDM is near-guaranteed, matching QPSK's `0:0` on 13,600 keys [E: `res-20261118-03`].

## 2. Where does the risk actually live, and will realizable posteriors move decisions?
- **OTFS co-delay (`rank S_W = m_0`, Astra's target) is doubly suppressed for realizable posteriors** [D, N]:
  - *Geometric `1/k_0` suppression*: Prop 1 Rem. 2 [E] gives `(Gamma_F^{OTFS} - u D_N)_{ij} = D_{k0}(dnu) sum_q (w_q - u/m_0) exp(2pi i dnu s_q / (m_0 k_0))`. For non-aliased `|dnu| < k_0`, every nonzero integer `dnu` gives `D_{k0}(dnu) = 0`; at fractional `dnu`, the row phase spread `2pi|dnu|/k_0` cancels `|D_{k0}| ~ |sin(pi dnu)|/(pi|dnu|)`, capping even point-mass adversarial error at `~1/k_0` (`0.030u` at `k_0=32`, `0.062u` at `k_0=16`, `0.13u` at `k_0=8`) [D, N]. Maximally adversarial fixed-`(mu, u)` 16-QAM (`1.8/0.2 E_s` half-row split) gives only `0.005-0.054 u` (`k_0=8-32`), shifting a zero-residual co-delay score by `0.02-0.09` (`k_0=8-16`) vs `G_D = 18.6-28.1` [N].
  - *Physical multipath row-symmetry*: In cyclic OTFS with distinct integer delays (`L < m_0`), paths map delay row `q` to disjoint rows `(q + l_i) mod m_0`, so `||H W e_{q,p}||^2 = sum|h_i|^2` is *identically constant* across `q` [D, N]. Multipath fading induces zero row structure: realizable i.i.d. 16-QAM gives `|Gamma_OTFS - u D_N|/u = 1e-4` to `6e-4` (`p95 <= 2e-3`) and score shifts `~1e-4` [N]. Deterministic 25% guard/pilot rows (identical in QPSK, not 16-QAM-specific) still shift scores by only `0.008-0.037` (`k_0=8, 16`) [N]. `O(u)` OTFS co-delay error (`0.51u` adv, `0.012u` i.i.d.) requires Doppler aliasing `|dnu| >= k_0` (or single-carrier `k_0 = 1`) [D, N]. **Likelihood that realizable non-aliased OTFS posteriors move real support decisions: very low (near zero)** [J].
- **Where realizable posteriors DO move decisions: OFDM (`a1 = 0` DAFT) *cross-delay* `Gamma_F - Gamma_C`** [D, N]:
  - For any time-flat `W` (AFDM/OFDM) on distinct delays, `h^H(Gamma_F - Gamma_C)h = sum_n (v_n - vbar)(g_n(h) - gbar) = N Cov_n(v_n, g_n(h))` (`<= 0` whenever `v_n` decreases with channel gain `g_n`) [D].
  - In OFDM (`a1 = 0`), frequency-selective fading concentrates `g_n = |H[n]|^2` at exact path-delay differences `d_{ij} = l_j - l_i`, creating coherent negative cross-delay error `h^H(Gamma_F - Gamma_C)h/u = -0.28` to `-0.60` (QPSK) and `-0.13` to `-0.43` (16-QAM) (`max|Gamma_cross|/u = 0.14-0.46`) [N]. On exact `N=256` 8-path low-Doppler (`|nu|<=0.2`) channels, `CODELAY` suffers `2.29x` worse gain NMSE (`0.0408` vs `0.0179`) and `9/40` (`22.5%`) full-delay-bank discordance vs `FULL`, flipping `~10%` of pair decisions [N]. Crucially, this is **cross-delay** (`rank S_W = 1` for OFDM) and **stronger in QPSK than 16-QAM** [N].
  - *(Side note)* Fractional-delay AFDM (`tau=0.5`) breaks column flatness (`[0.00, 2.17]`), but unitarity keeps row sums `= 1`, so i.i.d. `v_n` still averages out [D, N].

## 3. Cheapest decisive first check (<=0.5 person-day, <=1 core-hour; no 16-QAM decoder)
- **No 16-QAM decoder needed** [J]: production `F0`/BP/LDPC is QPSK-hardwired (`direction_receiver.py`, `experiments/sp_afdm_frozen_20260920/src/messages.py` `pd - |mu|^2`, `n=2048` LDPC) [E], whereas Prop 1-2 act on frozen `(mu, v)` inside exact `J_g^*(S)`. LMMSE equalization + exact 16-QAM MAP/BICM demapping + `J_g^*` scoring tests realizability directly without rewriting BP/LDPC.
- **Competing hypotheses & 3 arms** (1,000 fresh 8-path frames/cell, 7 retained + 1 missed weak path, full delay-Doppler bank):
  - **H1** (OTFS co-delay matters): realizable `v` beyond `(mu, u)` moves OTFS decisions and `ROW(w)` restores `FULL`.
  - **H0** (geometrically suppressed): flips require adversarial near-ties or `|dnu| >= k_0`.
  - **H2** (cross-delay fading comparator): real flips beyond `u` live in OFDM cross-delay, with QPSK >= 16-QAM.
  - **Arm A** (OTFS `64x16, 128x8`, adversarial fixed-`(mu, u)` 16-QAM inside `[v_min(mu), 1.8 E_s - |mu|^2]` + `+/-5%` perturbation): H1 predicts `>=5%` perturbation-stable flips restored by `ROW`; H0 predicts `<2%` collapsing under perturbation.
  - **Arm B** (OTFS `64x16, 128x8`, realizable LMMSE + 16-QAM soft demapper, SP-OTFS & 25% guard-row): H1 predicts `>=2%` `CODELAY`-vs-`ROW`/`FULL` step-0 discordance or `>=1 pp` net true-delay gain; H0 predicts `<0.5%` and `~0 pp`.
  - **Arm C** (positive control: realizable LMMSE QPSK & 16-QAM on OFDM `a1=0` vs AFDM `a1=5`): H2 predicts AFDM `<0.2%`, low-Doppler OFDM `>=5%` discordance with `FULL > CODELAY` and QPSK >= 16-QAM.
- **Kill criterion**: Kill the 16-QAM/OTFS co-delay line if Arm B shows `<1%` step-0 discordance or `<0.5 pp` net true-delay recovery gain (95% CI upper `<1 pp`) at non-aliased `k_0 in {8, 16}`, regardless of Arm A.
- **Weak (matrix-only) evidence**: nonzero `||Gamma_F - Gamma_C||_F`, `rank S_W > 1`, hand-tuned `Y` placed in a `~0.01u` tie, flips only at `|dnu| >= k_0` or vanishing under `+/-5%` noise, or breaking the QPSK-only `u_clamp` formula.

## 4. Sharper nearby free predictions for THIS paper (at most two)
1. **Chirp-controlled breakdown of single-scalar `CODELAY` (`a1 = 0` vs `1` vs `5` in THIS QPSK SP-AFDM codebase)** [D, N]:
   - *Risky prediction*: From `h^H(Gamma_F - Gamma_C)h = N Cov_n(v_n, g_n(h))` [D], setting `c1 = 0` (OFDM limit of the same DAFT transceiver) on low-Doppler (`|nu|<=0.25`) and `P1-G` channels breaks `CODELAY = FULL` (`median h^H(Gamma_F - Gamma_C)h/u <= -0.15`, `FULL` beats `CODELAY` in post-refit gain NMSE by `>=1.4x` and step-0 true-delay recovery), whereas turning on even `a1 = 1` (below Bemani's `5`) collapses `|Cov_n|` by `>10x` and restores `CODELAY = FULL` (`<0.5%` discordance, `1.00x` NMSE [N]).
   - *Why it could fail*: iterative LDPC BP mixes LLRs across subcarriers and may wash out fading correlation in `v_n` on undecoded frames, or detector absorption (`res-20261119-01`) may swamp the recovery lift.
   - *Cheapest check & kill*: existing QPSK `physical.py`/`compact_state.py`/LDPC changing only `c1 = a1/(2N)` (`a1 in {0, 1, 5}`), 400 fresh frames (`<=1` person-day, `<=3` core-hours). Kill if at `a1 = 0`, `median h^H(Gamma_F - Gamma_C)h/u > -0.05`, or `FULL - CODELAY` step-0 recovery `< +1.0 pp` (upper 95% CI `< 2 pp`) and NMSE ratio `< 1.15x`.
2. **Decoder-interface message type in frozen AFDM (`a1 = 5`): check-extrinsic vs APP (`raw_check_extrinsic`)** [E, J]:
   - *Risky prediction*: Passing LDPC check-extrinsic moments `(mu_ext, u_ext)` (`experiments/sp_afdm_frozen_20260920/src/receiver.py:64` [E]) instead of APP to `CSTATE` removes the Berkson self-feedback that shifted the forced-miss window `8x` (`xi_50 = 0.127`, `res-20261119-01` [E]) and drove `r_norm = 0.807` overfitting in DD `sigma^2` (`+1.64 dB`, `READOUTS.md` R-F, `ambition-direction-astra-20261119.md` §5 [E]): predicting `r_norm >= 0.95` on undecoded frames, halving absorption `|b1+b2|`, and cutting DD `sigma^2` loss by `>= 0.8 dB`.
   - *Why it could fail*: on undecoded frames `u_ext >> u_APP` (`E_ext << E_APP`), which can starve weak-path SNR and worsen `CODELAY` FER. (Novelty must stay scoped to support-exchange window & DD-`sigma^2` recovery, not inventing turbo extrinsic feedback [J].)
   - *Cheapest check & kill*: replay archived forced-miss & `sigma2_dd` triggers with `qsource='extrinsic_only'` (`<=0.5` person-day, `<=2` core-hours). Kill if `r_norm < 0.90`, absorption drop `< 30%`, or known-`sigma^2` `CODELAY` FER worsens by `> 1 pp`.
