## 1. Three additions ranked by impact per effort

**1. Test whether CODELAY predicts the uncertainty borne by the transmitted data. [Confident recommendation · high impact, moderate effort]** The 0.8% scalar-reward error answers an important question: the formula explains the score the receiver actually computes. A different question remains open. On decoder failures, does the receiver’s diagonal symbol-variance model assign the right physical uncertainty cost to a phantom candidate and a competing new-delay candidate? This is the addition I would choose. It tests the premise underneath both FULL and CODELAY, and the synthetic transmitter supplies the missing observable.

**[Derived]** Freeze one receiver state, candidate support and fitted gains before revealing the transmitted symbols. Let \(e=d-\mu\) be the realized data-symbol error in receiver units, \(V=\operatorname{diag}(v)\) the decoder’s reported marginal variances, and \(H\) the frozen candidate channel operator. The difference between realized and modeled distortion decomposes exactly:
\[
\|He\|^2-\operatorname{tr}(HVH^H)
=\sum_k (|e_k|^2-v_k)\|H_{:k}\|^2
 +2\operatorname{Re}\sum_{k<j}e_k^*e_j\,H_{:k}^HH_{:j}.
\]
The first term measures marginal-energy error. The second measures the contribution of cross-symbol products omitted by the diagonal model. A single frame supplies one realization. The signed average over fresh physical frames tests calibration in the chosen population.

**[Confident first step]** Freeze a small prospective replay at \(\rho=0.3\). On each triggered frame, select the best repeated-delay and new-delay candidates by one fixed receiver rule, with no truth labels. Hold their gains fixed. Difference the identity above between the two candidates, then compare the modeled and realized **cost contrasts** with a physical-frame paired interval. Report bias relative to the observed candidate score margins. The computation needs two forward applications \(He\) per pair, with no posterior sampler. **[Uncertain]** If the discrepancy is comparable to the margins, the split-invariance theorem still holds, but the physical interpretation of the diagonal decoder model needs narrowing. If it is small with a tight interval, the paper gains evidence that agreement with the receiver’s scorer alone cannot supply.

**3. Derive a sufficient condition for FULL and CODELAY to select the same edit. [Confident recommendation · moderate impact, low initial effort]** This would explain when discarding cross-delay terms preserves a *decision*. Observing identical FER leaves that question open.

\[
J_F-J_C=h_C^HDh_C-(Dh_C)^HQ_F^{-1}(Dh_C).
