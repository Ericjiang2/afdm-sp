## Frozen mechanism cohort and numerical conventions

The BASE-only JVP initial state is fixed by the production source: prior=entering.check, pd=1-system.rho, mu0/v0=moments(prior,pd), x0=pilot+mu0, h0=entering.gains, vh0=1/P, xb0=x0, hb0=h0, s0=sv0=0, absent previous bar/vp histories, and Y=IDAFT(y,c1,c2). For step t>0, recurrence inputs come from state[t-1]: post-step x/mu/v/h/vh, s, sv(=alpha), xbar/hbar and bar/vp histories. The current state[t] s/xbar/hbar/vr/vq fields are used only for this step's primal reconciliation and output readout, never as recurrence inputs. Reconcile each analytical primal step against the corresponding saved BASE fields and final unclipped LLR with fixed elementwise atol=1e-12 and rtol=1e-10; any failed comparison invalidates I and stops prediction admission. Predictor inputs are restricted to sealed observed/BASE data. Truth and intervention outputs are unavailable to prediction.

The population denominator is always256. Let T contain frames whose BASE native output triggers CSTATE and whose BASE final branch succeeds. The prediction-sealed mechanism cohort E consists exactly of frames in T for which max(abs(R-I))>1e-10 and **both** predicted unclipped2048-coordinate LLR-shift norms exceed1e-8. These numerical-zero thresholds, complete IDs and all exclusion reasons are sealed before intervention arms. Distinct-delay/zero-reaction frames and bypasses are retained as controls and in population outcomes; do not count bypasses as forced-update safety evidence. Any BASE numerical fallback is retained and prevents overall admission rather than being silently removed.

Primary transport uses all-I/all-Q unclipped LLR shifts, equivalent to natural fields via f=(L_I+iL_Q)/(4a). Compare predicted delta p_A with actual delta a_A=L_A-L_BASE for A=CGR,SHAM. Per-frame normalized error is ||p_A-a_A||/||a_A|| and cosine is the real inner product divided by norms. Pooled values concatenate complete frame vectors; label these energy-weighted summaries separately from framewise inference. Zero observed norm gives an explicit undefined ratio, not deletion; raw squared errors still enter contrasts. A zero pooled denominator makes that representation inconclusive.

Use the source-derived one-sided derivative at exact floor/clipping boundaries. Also compare derivative-clipped predictions to actual saved clipped decoder-input shifts. Do not substitute a nonlinear clip of a finite predicted LLR for the declared derivative. Report boundary counts and both representations.

For each frame and arm define q_own=||a_A-p_A||², q_zero=||a_A||² and q_swap=||a_A-p_other||². Own-versus-zero and own-versus-swapped differences are q_zero-q_own and q_swap-q_own. A numerical tie satisfies abs(diff)<=1e-10*max(1,q_own,q_reference). For each of the four **unclipped** contrasts, use a one-sided exact sign test P[Binomial(wins+losses,.5)>=wins]; ties are reported separately. Apply Holm correction across these four p-values at family alpha=.05. No post-hoc selection of metrics, representation, arm or direction.

## Fixed gates and classifications

**I — integrity/identifiability:** all256 observations and768 attempts complete under the locked sources/streams; prediction-before-intervention and observed-before-truth Git seals valid; zero unexpected numerical fallback; native/entering pairing exact; unchanged receiver semantics; actual decoder input=clip(detector extrinsic). **M — prospective transport:** E has at least8 frames, and each of the four sign tests has at least8 nontied frames; all four Holm-adjusted p-values<=.05. Each arm's pooled unclipped error ratio<1 and cosine>0. Its pooled derivative-clipped error also beats zero and swapped prediction and has positive cosine. All values and ties remain reported if the gate is inconclusive. Pooled ratios>=1, negative orientation or own-arm failing to distinguish swapped is direct adverse evidence; failure of statistical/minimum-n criteria alone means insufficient support, not a proof of absence.

**U — bounded decision/safety:** over all256 frames, CGR rescues at least2 BASE information-word failures, harms zero BASE-correct frames, produces zero new valid-but-wrong words, and its net rescues exceed SHAM's net rescues by at least1. Recount information bits using actual free_cols and coded errors/syndromes separately. Report all pairwise2×2 tables and exact one-sided McNemar p-values/confidence summaries; passing this finite engineering gate does not establish a population FER improvement. If fewer than2 BASE failures occur, mark recovery evidence insufficient; do not enrich or top up frames.

**L — message calibration:** using actual clipped final decoder inputs and coded truth, loss=mean(logaddexp(0,-(1-2*c)*L)) per frame. Require mean CGR-minus-BASE loss<0 on E, mean CGR-minus-SHAM loss<0 on E, and mean CGR-minus-BASE loss<=0 over all256. These intervals describe precision and are not substituted for the fixed point gates. Bits are never independent resampling units; lower log-loss is not necessary for an individual rescue.
