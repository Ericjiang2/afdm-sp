## (b) Ranked shortlist (~10, from v1 + v2) most decisive for judging how A' differs from prior work

1. **F4-01 Kanazawa et al.** (FULL read). Same system class (LDPC-coded SP-OTFS, CE + MP detection + LDPC decoder loop, replica-based cancellation). Static r_unc/r_cod/r_end switch between uncoded and coded iterations; Sec. VI states adaptive stopping on "LLR variance or residual interference power" as future work. Could read all; could not see the VTC 2023 precursor.
2. **C1-01 / C1-02 Ylioinas (TCOM 2013 + 2010 thesis).** Channel estimator treated as a schedulable block alongside detector and decoder; EXIT + trellis-search activation schedule; offline vs per-frame unclear. Only abstract-level, second-hand; IEEE full text not read.
3. **F2-01 Lee & Yang (TCOM 2010).** Turbo equalizer + LDPC decoder; monitors iterative status to decide whether another equalization pass runs. Closest match to the "decide per frame whether to run another estimation/detection pass" mechanism. Only second-hand abstract paraphrase; full text CLOSED.
4. **C2-01 Amador et al. (Hybrid Iteration Control).** Early declaration of undecodable blocks from satisfied-check count + erased-message metric; explicit false-alarm / missed-detection tuning; static thresholds; decoder-only. Full text read.
5. **F3-01 Strodthoff et al.** Per-codeword failure prediction from decoder-progress features; FNR/FPR working point, effective BLER 1e-5 target, threshold moving. Full text read; no iterative receiver / no estimation loop.
6. **C1-10 Chen, Maunder, Hanzo (early stopping + deferred iteration, turbo HARQ).** MI-gain stop and EXIT-tunnel-based deferral, decision = continue vs request more information, 50-85% complexity reductions with no PLR loss. Abstract-level via search summaries only (Southampton 403).
7. **C3-02 Thaj & Viterbo.** Frame-level LDPC parity stop of turbo iterations in LDPC-coded OTFS (the "syndrome early stopping" baseline analog). Full text grep; channel known (CE-in-loop not checked).
8. **F5-04 Samsung WCNC 2021.** ML picks stop iteration vs parity-check ET; 30%-36% (LDPC) at 10% BLER, negligible BLER loss. Landing page only: authors, features, thresholding not available.
9. **C4-05 Akyildiz & Mahdavifar** (+ **C4-02 Jazbec et al.**, **C4-01 CALM**, **C4-03 LTT / CRC**). Cost-vs-risk stopping rule (lambda trade-off) and distribution-free threshold calibration for early exit. Abstracts + stopping section read; none is an iterative receiver.
10. **C2-06 Li, Shen, Tse** and **C2-07 Wang, Yedidia, Draper.** Escalation of decoding effort conditional on CRC/convergence failure (list size up; stage activation). Abstract-level; not receivers with estimation loops.
Also useful context: F2-02 Haddad (FULL; offline EXIT schedule, <0.15 dB loss, remark that combining with early stopping "might diminish the benefit"), C1-08 Wu et al. (FULL; CE inner iterations 5 then 1), P-01/P-02 patents (per-block iteration selection from SIR / MSE threshold; patent text only).
