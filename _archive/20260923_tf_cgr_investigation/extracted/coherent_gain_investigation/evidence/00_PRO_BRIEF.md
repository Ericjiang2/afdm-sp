# TF+CSTATE research-discovery brief

This is an evidence map for an independent research investigation, not a receiver proposal or novelty assessment. The complete fresh population is 3,072 independently generated observations, 1,024 each at 6, 6.5, and 7 dB. Only 6.5 dB was the preregistered confirmatory cell. The frozen TF+CSTATE receiver is the strongest tested receiver here: information-word failures were 61, 6, and 5, versus 206, 40, and 20 for frozen TF. Paired rescues were 145, 34, and 15 with zero observed harms. At 6.5 dB, the registered conservative one-sided 95% upper bound on the paired FER difference was −0.01950560. These are results on this sealed simulated population, not universal performance claims. See `sources/prospective_final_report.md` and `sources/prospective_verification_receipt.json`.

The original unattended analyzer rejected an extra Git-binding receipt field **before truth access**, so its campaign status remains `invalid`. User-authorized offline recovery on the same sealed data completed, and the original independent verifier passed without new receiver calls. Thus the preregistered **numerical** rule passed with a procedural qualification, not as an unqualified clean one-shot run. The 6 and 7 dB cells remain descriptive.

## What the 72 remaining failures look like

All 72 are triggered TF errors that CSTATE did not rescue: 61 at 6 dB, 6 at 6.5 dB, 5 at 7 dB. The same campaign has 194 rescued triggered controls and 2,806 easy nontriggered TF successes. On the 72 failures, CSTATE's saved objective improved in 72/72 and at least one structural move was accepted in 68/72, yet the information word stayed wrong. This coexistence is not proof that the objective caused the failure. The complete 515 saved structural steps, each with the full slot × 2,575 score grid and top-20 geometries, reside under `cstate_landscape/`; `truth_candidate_ranks.jsonl` is post-seal and never influenced selection.

The strongest descriptive distinctions from the rescued triggered cohort are: receiver-observed baseline syndrome median **169.5 versus 115.5**; post-seal final saved-gain operator NMSE median **0.0139 versus 0.0067**; and the best complex-gain projection **at each selected final geometry** median NMSE **0.0041 versus 0.0018**. True repeated-delay paths occur in **37/72 versus 65/194**; at the confirmatory 6.5 dB cell they occur in **6/6 versus 21/34**, a small outcome-selected comparison. Working-geometry missing distinct delays occur in **30/72 failures versus 114/194 rescues**, and also **1,286/2,806 easy successes**: missing delay alone is neither a sufficient failure explanation nor a deployable trigger. These comparisons are descriptive and mix SNRs; per-SNR distributions and tails are in `cohort_comparison.md` and `fresh_frame_table.csv`.

Selected-geometry projection NMSE is at most 0.01 in **64/72** remaining failures, but the exact global optimum over the fixed-cardinality CSTATE model class was **not computed**. A good gain refit for a saved geometry does not prove that a receiver can find or decode it. Similarly, at least one true-path nearest candidate fell outside the first-step top 20 in **64/72 failures and 171/194 rescues**, so this pathwise rank flag alone does not separate outcomes. Raw per-path and per-step ranks, including frozen tie rules, are retained for deeper inspection.

## Questions left open by the frozen evidence

1. **Representability:** how much residual would the globally best legal fixed-cardinality geometry leave? The pack has rigorous selected-geometry projections and bounds, not that global optimum. Inspect `truth_diagnostics/frame_diagnostics.jsonl` and `truth_diagnostics/path_diagnostics.jsonl`.
2. **Structural evidence/search:** when an adequate geometry exists, does the frozen score rank and two-move trajectory expose it? Inspect full `cstate_landscape/<frame>.npz`, `cstate_landscape.jsonl`, and `truth_candidate_ranks.jsonl`. A nearest single-path rank is not a joint-geometry reachability proof.
3. **In-span inference versus communication objective:** saved-gain mismatch often exceeds the fixed-geometry refit residual, and all remaining failures coexist with score improvement. Exact causal attribution requires more than these associations. Inspect `tf_traces/<frame>.npz`, `truth_diagnostics/`, and matched `remaining_failure_dossiers/<frame>.json`.
4. **Low-information cases:** no fresh matched true-geometry/full-H oracle receiver outputs were saved. Older outcome-selected oracle interventions cannot label these 72 frames irreducible. Inspect the historical ledger separately.

The older selected 6.5 dB audit weakened “a few more ordinary TF iterations/rounds” (9/32 rescued in the union of two finite-budget arms), found no extra rescue from a wider true-data Doppler window (20/32 in either window), and found no conditional-to-refit/marginal true-word preference reversal (0/32) on the tested words. Scalar gain-variance, passive whitening/GCV, tangent/covariance screens, and the toy non-Gaussian leakage result are also bounded or inconclusive; see `negative_results_ledger.md` for exact designs and scope. None of these older cohorts is pooled into the fresh FER denominator.

## Evidence boundary

`frame_table.jsonl` and saved NPZ observations are receiver-observable. Outcome cohorts, generated physical truth, projection errors, and truth-relative ranks are post-seal and **not deployable receiver inputs**. Controls were chosen within SNR using observed syndrome and TF runtime only, after outcome-defined cohort eligibility. Full per-iteration detector state vectors, complete LDPC-message/syndrome trajectories, and per-candidate timing were never recorded and remain unavailable. `availability_map_final.md` and the hash manifests give exact availability and provenance. No receiver, generator, or new structural search was run for this pack.
