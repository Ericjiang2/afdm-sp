% Drop-in paragraph for Section 3 "UWA channel setup", replacing the existing
% one-liner about the choice of N = 512.  This version is kappa-driven: it
% explains *why* shorter frames such as N = 256 or N = 128 are infeasible for
% this specific UWA configuration, without claiming a universal lower bound.

The choice of $N = 512$ is not a convenience default: it is dictated by the
Doppler quantisation that governs the Dirichlet $k$-leak expansion of
Section~\ref{ssec:frac_doppler}.  With $v_\mathrm{mean} = 2$\,m/s,
$f_c = 12$\,kHz, $c_\mathrm{sound} = 1500$\,m/s, and $f_s = 8$\,kHz the
normalised Doppler of the typical path is
$D \;=\; v f_c / (c_\mathrm{sound} f_s) \;\approx\; 0.002$,
so the real-valued Doppler bin index is $k_\mathrm{real} = DN = 0.002\,N$.
The Dirichlet expansion is dominated by a \emph{single} virtual tap when the
fractional residual $\kappa = k_\mathrm{real} - \mathrm{round}(k_\mathrm{real})$
is close to zero, and spreads each physical path over the full $2K_\mathrm{leak}+1 = 5$
virtual taps when $|\kappa|$ approaches $0.5$.  The virtual tap count
$P_\mathrm{actual}$ therefore satisfies $P_\mathrm{actual} \approx P$ in the
near-integer regime and $P_\mathrm{actual} \approx (2K_\mathrm{leak}+1)P$ in
the worst fractional regime.

For this UWA configuration the three candidate frame lengths fall at very
different points of that curve:

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{cccccc}
\toprule
$N$ & $k_\mathrm{real} = 0.002N$ & $|\kappa|$ & $P_\mathrm{actual}$ (typical) & Full-diversity $\delta_\mathrm{max} < N$? & Joint BER at 20\,dB \\
\midrule
$128$ & $0.256$ & $0.256$ & $\approx 45$--$50$ & Yes ($\delta_\mathrm{max} = 62$) & $\approx 0.22$--$0.29$ \\
$256$ & $0.512$ & $0.488$\textbf{(worst)}    & $\approx 43$--$44$ & Yes ($\delta_\mathrm{max} = 127$) & $\approx 0.18$--$0.21$ \\
$512$ & $1.024$ & $0.024$ & $\approx 17$--$33$ & Yes ($\delta_\mathrm{max} = 252$) & $\approx 0.01$--$0.05$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

With the canonical UWA parameters, $N = 256$ is within a few bins of the
half-integer worst case ($\kappa \approx 0.49$), so every physical path
spills into the full five-tap Dirichlet neighbourhood and the sparse-support
assumption breaks: OMP is forced to estimate $\sim 50$ taps from
$Q \approx 130$ dictionary columns, and the joint receiver loses its
advantage over the serial baselines.  $N = 128$ lands at $\kappa \approx 0.26$
with the same symptom.  $N = 512$ places the typical path at
$k_\mathrm{real} \approx 1.02$, i.e.\ barely off the integer grid, so
$P_\mathrm{actual}$ stays close to $P$ and the sparse-support assumption
continues to hold.  Choosing a different $N$ would require either a lower
velocity regime or a larger sampling rate to restore the near-integer
condition; within the canonical UWA configuration $N = 512$ is the smallest
frame length that keeps the Dirichlet expansion benign.
