# 6.5 dB receiver-identity audit (read-only)

**Finding: C — different receiver semantics in the proposed 40/1024 versus 0/256 contrast.** The old 40 failures are the **TF** arm, whose final decision is the frozen two-round native decoder. The new **BASE** arm is a TF+CSTATE receiver: it runs a compact CSTATE refit and a fresh 20-iteration detector/LDPC round whenever the final native syndrome is nonzero. The appropriate old receiver comparator is **TF_CSTATE, 6/1024** at 6.5 dB, subject separately to population comparability; the 40/1024 TF rate must not be inserted into a zero-of-256 probability calculation for BASE. This is a receiver-definition correction, not evidence that the CGR candidate passed.
