## Closest-art check (primary sources; narrow scope)

1. **Bilinear AMP ancestry — Jason T. Parker & Philip Schniter, “Parametric Bilinear Generalized Approximate Message Passing,” arXiv:1508.07575 (2015).** Its derivation makes a tractable sum-product simplification under an i.i.d. Gaussian three-way tensor / large-system model. This establishes prior bilinear-AMP and large-system approximation ancestry; it does not state the AFDM-specific Γ(V) identity or this receiver's finite-operator defect. [Author paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.07575). Local implementation records the ancestry and says its coherent variant retains weighted off-diagonal terms: `results/tf_cgr_mechanism_20260923/qualification/local_20260923_v4/archived_reference_copy/frozen_reference/native/src/fast_bilinear.py:1-5` (SHA-256 `873a83ff22c76b7c087c4e1aad3f7a5e6079be981b13e79a9462819ef00a9f12`).
2. **Non-i.i.d. operator AMP — Sundeep Rangan, Philip Schniter & Alyson K. Fletcher, “Vector Approximate Message Passing,” arXiv:1610.03082 (2016; IEEE TIT 2019).** The paper contrasts AMP's i.i.d. sub-Gaussian state-evolution setting with VAMP's right-orthogonally-invariant matrix class. It supports operator-geometry-aware AMP ancestry, not a guarantee for this deterministic AFDM operator absent its assumptions. [Author paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.03082).
3. **EP and correlated beliefs — Thomas P. Minka, “Expectation Propagation for approximate Bayesian inference,” arXiv:1301.2294 (author version).** The paper describes moment-based projection and richer belief states that can incorporate correlations. This blocks any claim that preserving correlations is new in general; it does not supply the project-specific path-Gram contraction. [Author paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.2294).
4. **Off-grid parameterized geometry — Zai Yang, Lihua Xie & Cishen Zhang, “Off-grid Direction of Arrival Estimation Using Sparse Bayesian Inference,” arXiv:1108.5838 (2011; IEEE TSP 2012).** It explicitly models deviations from a discretized angular grid and iteratively estimates them with Bayesian sparse inference. This is an off-grid geometry precedent, not AFDM delay-Doppler structural reopening. [Author paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.5838).
5. **Continuous/gridless geometry — Gongguo Tang, Badri Narayan Bhaskar, Parikshit Shah & Benjamin Recht, “Compressed Sensing off the Grid,” arXiv:1207.6053 (2012).** The paper treats frequencies as continuous, not restricted to a discrete grid, via atomic-norm recovery. This defeats broad novelty claims about continuous parameter geometry; it is not a coded iterative AFDM receiver. [Author paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.6053).
6. **Coded joint channel inference — Peng Sun, Zhongyong Wang, Robert W. Heath Jr. & Philip Schniter, “Joint Channel-Estimation/Decoding with Frequency-Selective Channels and Few-Bit ADCs,” arXiv:1807.02494 (2018).** It uses P-BiG-AMP for joint channel estimation, equalization, and soft decoding in coded single-carrier transmission. This is a close coded-bilinear receiver precedent; its waveform/ADC/model differs and it does not pre-empt the exact AFDM/CPP consistency statement. [Author paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.02494).
