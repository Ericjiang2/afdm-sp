PART I — VERIFY THE NEW CLOSURE-COMPATIBILITY THEORY
======================================================================

The research reassessment proposes the following extension of the already audited identity

    Gamma_ij(V) = tr(U_i^H U_j V).

For unitary path operators U_1,...,U_P, consider the scalar identity closure

    Gamma_theta(V) = tr(V) I.

The proposed claim is:

For a covariance family V_family, this closure is exact for every
V in V_family iff

    tr(U_i^H U_j V) = 0

for every i != j and every V in V_family.

Three proposed specializations are:

1. For all isotropic V = v I:

       tr(U_i^H U_j) = 0.

2. For all diagonal V = diag(v) in a specified coordinate system:

       diag(U_i^H U_j) = 0.

3. For all positive-semidefinite V:

       U_i^H U_j = 0.

Because U_i^H U_j is unitary for two distinct unitary path operators,
the third condition cannot hold for two or more paths.

The reassessment also proposes that for a same-delay doublet,

    R = [[1, rho],
         [rho*, 1]]

with

    r = |rho| = |D_N(nu_2 - nu_1)|,

the eigenvalues are

    1-r, 1+r,

hence

    ||R-I||_2 = r

and

    min_c ||R-cI||_2 = r.

Therefore no scalar retuning cI can uniformly reproduce both eigendirections.

Finally, for fixed noninteger Doppler-bin separation delta, verify the claimed asymptotic form of D_N(delta), including the exact phase/sign convention of the frozen AFDM operator.

3. correct any false iff statement, coordinate dependence, Hermitian/complex subtlety, or asymptotic phase/sign issue;
4. connect it exactly to the already verified project identities;
5. state the strongest safe proposition that should appear in the manuscript.

Important:

- distinguish arbitrary Hermitian/PSD V from arbitrary complex matrices;

Numerically verify the proposition with finite-dimensional tests using the frozen AFDM/CPP operators.
