# 1. Current scientific question

The current front-end path is approximately:

$$
\text{515 integer DD pilot atoms}
\rightarrow
\text{up to 40 selected atoms}
\rightarrow
\text{delay-energy grouping}
\rightarrow
\le 8\text{ retained delay groups}
\rightarrow
\text{one Doppler center per delay}
\rightarrow
\text{bounded physical refinement}
\rightarrow
\text{TF}.
$$

CSTATE is invoked only after decoder failure and can globally reopen physical model space, including insertion of previously absent delays and distinct same-delay Dopplers.

Existing mechanism evidence shows that CSTATE repairs real structural failures. However, it is not yet known whether those failures were:

1. genuinely not discoverable from the initial pilot observation at modest cost; or
2. already visible in the coarse acquisition but discarded by grouping / slot allocation / one-center-per-delay compression.

This distinction is now critical to the paper.

A cheap stronger front end that eliminates most CSTATE rescues would materially weaken the current systems argument.
