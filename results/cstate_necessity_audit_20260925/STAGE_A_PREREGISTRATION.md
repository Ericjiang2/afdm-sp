## Fixed endpoints and contrasts

**Primary necessity contrast:** D versus C eventual **information-word FER** over all 1,024; report exact paired cells `both correct, C-only correct, D-only correct, both wrong`, signed `FER_D−FER_C`, conservative one-sided Clopper–Pearson component bound, and exact discordant-pair test. **Primary stronger-front-end substitution contrast:** C versus B on the same full denominator and signed `FER_C−FER_B`. Also report B−A and C−A, all six pairwise overlaps, coded BER, syndrome, valid-but-wrong, and the prespecified original-40 labels. These contrasts are developmental diagnostics; no p-value alone promotes a publication claim.

Timing starts before input load/hash for each arm and ends after durable decision/raw/checkpoint serialization, with ledger append separately declared exactly as in the original campaign. Report complete mean/median/p95/p99/max, acquisition-only time, duplicate-preserving refit, TF, LDPC, trigger/branch, serialization, peak state/RSS, source-cache preparation and amortization, numerical failures, and **all-denominator** latest-ready checkpoint correctness/missing at 0.5 and 1.0 s. A triggered-subset mean does not replace an all-frame mean or p95. Include `P_F0/P_F1`, F1 same-delay count, front-end pilot-only rank, later APP-conditioned rank (where archived), CSTATE replacement class, and Part-A lineage label/availability without converting unknowns into zeros.

For a generic signed paired difference `d=FER_X−FER_Y=(h−r)/1024`, `h` is Y-correct/X-wrong and `r` is Y-wrong/X-correct. Use `U_CP(h/1024; tail=.025)−L_CP(r/1024; tail=.025)` as a conservative one-sided upper bound. For **additional D gain** define `g=FER_C−FER_D`; its upper bound switches rescue/harm accordingly. The fixed **1 percentage-point** tolerance below is an engineering scope threshold for this developmental population, not a universal equivalence margin. No outcome-driven threshold, frame exclusion, SNR change or extra arm may be introduced after observing C/D.

## Predeclared developmental disposition

1. **Broad CSTATE necessity story fails on this tested regime** only if C correctly decodes at least **26 of the original 34 B-only rescues** (≥75%), C-versus-B excess-FER upper bound is **<1 percentage point**, the upper bound on any D-over-C gain is **<1 percentage point**, and C's observed complete mean and p95 are **no larger than B's** under the matched new clocks. This is a scoped risk-cost conclusion for this reused population, never global equivalence or proof of a zero D effect.
2. **Scoped engineering value** if F1 absorbs meaningful original rescues but fails at least one cost/uncertainty condition above, or D retains an uncertain incremental effect. Describe its observed trigger/cost frontier; do not force a binary scientific win from a nonsignificant D−C p-value.
3. **Incremental CSTATE evidence after stronger acquisition** if the conservative upper bound on `FER_D−FER_C` is **<0** on the full paired population, with no unexpected integrity failure. Report complete and 0.5-s risk-cost separately; the result would be developmental support, still not fresh confirmation.
4. **F2 planning may be justified**, not executed, if F1 fails to recover a majority of the original B-only rescues **and** the independent acquisition-lineage audit supports an early-information/representation gap that F1's physical pilot-only search could not close. If Part A is A4 (insufficient archive), Stage A alone cannot assign that cause. An unresolved source/adapter qualification or excessive F1 cost is a blocker to interpreting F1 scientifically, not evidence that F2 would work.

These cases are not an exhaustive partition. In particular, a wide D−C interval, F1 numerical failures, or a faster-but-less-reliable C remains **inconclusive** on necessity. Do not use the 32 selected old failures, early CSTATE APP scores, or oracle true geometry to fill missing full-population outcomes.
