# CSTATE acquisition-lineage audit (archive only)

**Verdict: A4 — archive insufficient to decide initial pilot visibility.** CSTATE demonstrably repaired late-stage structural geometry on the final prospective sample, but neither the final saved raw schema nor the older Part-A archive preserves the ordered pilot-only selected atoms, coefficients and residuals required to decide whether the useful directions were initially visible and then discarded. Later APP-conditioned structural ranks cannot stand in for initial pilot ranks. This is a limitation of the archive, not evidence for A1 or A2. Calls made here: **0 receiver, 0 decoder, 0 acquisition replay, 0 new observations**.

## Actual source chain and retained fields

The user's “up to 40 selected atoms” was approximate. Registered [`pilot_front`](../../experiments/sp_afdm_frozen_20260920/src/receiver.py) (lines 122–129) constructs 103 delays × five integer Dopplers `−2..2` = **515** pilot regressors, with candidate index `5l+(k+2)`, calls `B0-p2-s0.5` at capacity 40, and returns only selected delay/Doppler/gain state to native TF. Registered [`supplement_baselines.py`](../../experiments/sp_afdm_frozen_20260920/upstream/spafdm_receiver_handoff/afdm_sp_joint_unrolled/supplement_baselines.py) (lines 24–38, 137–147) maps B0 index 2 to **24** OMP nominations, despite capacity 40; its `record` has `omp_selected` but not a persisted per-frame candidate-rank/residual trace. The dictionary is generated using `Channel.regressors(observed['pilot'])`, then passed with normalized observed `y` as complex64 to the initializer. [`omp_sparse_torch`](../../experiments/sp_afdm_frozen_20260920/upstream/spafdm_receiver_handoff/afdm_sp_joint_unrolled/classical_joint_unrolled.py) (lines 138–157) selects maximum raw `|Phiᴴ residual|` and refits with least squares, with no per-column unit-norm normalization in that function; the received observation and pilot are scaled together in the prospective generator. The exact source SHA values, checked against final registration, are in [artifact availability](artifact_availability.json).

Registered [`twofft.time_refine`](../../experiments/sp_afdm_native_joint_20260921/src/twofft.py) (lines 82–109) groups the acquired atoms by integer delay, ranks delay groups by sum of squared coarse gains, retains at most eight, and chooses exactly one strongest integer Doppler center for each retained delay. It optimizes each center only within `center ±0.75`, clipped to `−3..3`, over two coordinate passes. [`native_twofft.receive`](../../experiments/sp_afdm_native_joint_20260921/src/native_twofft.py) (lines 16–35) repeats this for two coded rounds and records *post-refit* paths, not the pilot OMP list. The historical Part-A producer explicitly wrote `initial_acquisition_paths=None` and [unavailable_fields.json](../tf_production_gap_audit_20260922/A/unavailable_fields.json) states that pilot-front state was not retained. The final prospective [raw payload schema](../../experiments/tf_cstate_prospective_20260923/run_prospective.py) (lines 304–317) likewise saves native arrays and CSTATE geometry, but not pilot-front selected atoms/order/coarse residual.

The later structural bank is a **different** search: [`geometry_validation._bank`](../../experiments/tf_cstate_validation_20260922/geometry_validation.py) (lines 101–108) makes 103 delays × 25 quarter-bin Dopplers = 2,575 candidates. CSTATE may make at most two global slot replacements (lines 325–375), followed by local refinement. Those candidate scores use APP-derived data moments, so an inserted delay or a high late rank does not prove pilot-only visibility.

In later GLOBAL20 steps, `f0035` first replaced delay 75/ν1.466 by delay 51/ν1.0 (objective decrease 28.418); this grid candidate is near the archived true delay-51 path at ν1.008110. `f0221` first inserted delay 51/ν0.75 in place of delay 94/ν−2.262 (decrease 7.767), then replaced an existing delay-37/ν0.753 slot by delay 37/ν0.75 (decrease 0.019); that second grid candidate is near the archived true delay-37 path at ν0.732984. Their final continuous-refinement coordinates are not in the compact step table, so the true-path ν values must not be mislabeled as refined estimates. `f0863` created a second delay-36 fitted hypothesis yet was not rescued; `f0605` was not rescued. All these are **later APP-conditioned** events. The replacement table retains their exact full hashes and step values. No statement that the same physical direction ranked highly at pilot acquisition follows.

**Necessity boundary.** The frozen front end imposes a one-center-per-delay representation before TF; the late structural search can reopen absent delays or add distinct same-delay fitted paths. This is a real architectural bottleneck in the source. The archive does not show how many of the 34 final CSTATE rescues arose from coarse pilot evidence that the bottleneck discarded, versus directions revealed only after decoded APP information. In particular, a late same-delay pair can be a true doublet, one-path leakage approximation, or model-mismatch hypothesis; without A1–A3 and per-frame final geometry/truth alignment here those alternatives remain unresolved. **A4** is the only supported mechanism verdict.
