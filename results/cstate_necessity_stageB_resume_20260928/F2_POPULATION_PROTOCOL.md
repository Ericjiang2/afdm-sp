No truth opens in the producer.

Each frame executes **one current-environment F0+TF two-round prefix** and **one current-environment F2E+TF two-round prefix**. The two prefix orders alternate by frame index (512 each), and both prefixes, including two durable checkpoints, raw and decision serialization, finish before any continuation. The F0 prefix is checked against its sealed Stage-A A native arrays/bits/syndrome outside the policy clock; its nonzero-syndrome population must match the original 40 IDs. Each F0 failure forks deep-copied native APP/check into B/CSTATE, matched LOCAL and triggered F2T, each with one legal TF20/LDPC50 continuation. Each F2E failure forks its own deep-copied APP/check into LOCAL and, **only if the P155 CSTATE branch qualified**, CSTATE. Branch order rotates by triggered-frame ordinal; F0 and F2E branch groups reverse on alternate frames. No branch can mutate another's prefix.

The full 1024-frame logical policies are `F0`, `B=F0+CSTATE`, `F0_LOCAL`, `F2T=F0+triggered F2`, `F2E`, `F2E_LOCAL`, and optionally `F2E_CSTATE`. A nontriggered policy returns its own prefix decision. **Actual executions** are exactly 2048 prefix wrappers, 120 F0 continuations if all 40 original triggers reproduce, and at most 1024 or 2048 F2E continuations depending on CSTATE admission and observed F2E triggers. **Logical outcomes** are 1024 rows per admitted policy. Actual calls and logical policy rows are never conflated.

Each logical policy's complete latency equals the entire durable prefix cost from its own attempt origin (input load, receiver, checkpoint/raw/decision serialization) plus its **own** complete continuation cost (deep copy, attempt journal, geometry/F2, TF, decoder, checkpoint/raw/decision serialization). Waiting while another policy branch runs is excluded. Its 0.5/1.0-s decisions use the latest durable prefix checkpoint or its own durable branch checkpoint on this logical timeline. Components retain actual mutually exclusive F2 estimator timings, native stage/geometry diagnostics, TF/decoder and serialization; report both triggered conditional and 1024-frame population branch costs. Preflight inventory, historical parity reading and final seal hashing are experimental validation overhead outside individual policy clocks and reported separately.

The producer writes attempt journal **before** each actual call, per-call raw/checkpoints/decision and ledger, `policy_ledger.jsonl`, and an observed seal only after complete success. The locked manifest fixes a positive per-call operational cap for every admitted arm and a total wall cap after the saved-state qualifications; caps do not change the scientific method. Any source/hash/schema/numerical/I/O error or wall cap stops as `invalid_partial`, preserving charged attempts and ready checkpoints; no completed-subset FER.
