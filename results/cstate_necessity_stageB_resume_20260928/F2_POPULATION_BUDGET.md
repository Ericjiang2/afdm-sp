# F2 full developmental run — authorized operational cap

The authorized campaign is **one** 1024-frame pass over the already sealed 6.5-dB cohort. It executes 2048 paid F0/F2E prefixes, 120 continuations if the original 40 F0 triggers reproduce (B, F0+LOCAL, F2T), and two continuations for each observed F2E trigger (LOCAL and qualified CSTATE), at most 2048. Thus the maximum actual complete wrapper/continuation count is 4216; each of the seven logical policies still has 1024 outcome rows. No extra observation, tuning, or replay is allowed.

The three saved-state qualification calls completed with no truth read: F2T 16.6105 s, F2E+LOCAL 51.7174 s, F2E+CSTATE 52.2074 s. The previous one-frame F2E v2 receiver plus durable output was about 16 s, while the prior Stage-A A prefix averaged 0.3493 s over 1024 saved calls. If **all** frames trigger F2E and those single-frame times transfer, F2E prefix plus its two branches would take about `(16+51.7174+52.2074)×1024/3600 ≈ 34.1 h`, before current F0 and 120 F0 continuations. This is a capacity estimate, not a timing or reliability guarantee: trigger count and frame-dependent P155 costs remain unknown.

The frozen total campaign wall cap is **72 h after preflight**, with a 72 h + 5 min launcher cap for preflight/cleanup. Each actual call has a separately frozen operational cap: F0/F2E/F2T 120 s; B/F0+LOCAL/F2E+LOCAL/F2E+CSTATE 900 s. These are stop rules, not estimator iteration or method parameters. Hitting any cap, source/data mismatch or numerical/I/O failure yields `invalid_partial`, preserves attempted and ready-checkpoint cost, and forbids a completed-subset FER claim. No automatic retry or per-frame substitution is permitted. The F1 2112-wrapper campaign has different instrumentation and remains separate; compare B timing within each campaign.
