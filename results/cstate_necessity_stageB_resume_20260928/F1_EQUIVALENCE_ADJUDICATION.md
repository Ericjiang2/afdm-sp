**Decision for the next run:** keep the frozen Stage-A exact-counter gate and `atol=2e−10, rtol=0` continuous/bit-exact gates. ## What the evidence establishes

The stopped `s650_f0197` raw records `coordinate_scores=1002`; Stage-A reference and three later F1-only and full-history diagnostics record 995. Its saved final Dopplers/gains/objective history differ by at most `6.56e−11`/`9.14e−11`/`1.88e−10`; final bits match, but the old producer failed at the counter gate before certifying every downstream field. The old and new f197 raw have small continuous differences, including r0 APP max `2.58e−10`, in a zero-receiver offline comparison. The original process's **first intermediate coordinate divergence was not saved**. Address-alignment checks of terminal bank/Gram/solve and terminal `score`/`score_derivatives` were bitwise equal; neither reconstructs earlier candidate values or that process state. The later full 409-call original schedule finished with all saved reference gates passing. Thus no available evidence uniquely distinguishes evaluation order, floating-point branch/tie, cache/stride layout, or another runtime influence. No exact root-cause category is claimed.

## Algorithm versus implementation-order counter

The physical search contract fixes the 2,575-atom bank and ascending tie order, eight-path cap, 80-coordinate-visit/full-eight budget, four Newton steps/visit, duplicate predicate, bounded line search, joint-ridge objective, acceptance/stopping tests, retained slots and native TF/LDPC continuation. Ordered selected paths, stop reason, accepted objective history, final channel and decoded bits are algorithm-facing outputs. `coordinate_scores` increments on a **new floating-key lookup** in the local `evaluated` dictionary. Values proposed by Newton/line search can differ at roundoff scale even when the accepted channel remains within the frozen continuous tolerance; the number of distinct evaluated keys then changes. That makes this count partly an implementation-order/cost artifact. However it was explicitly frozen as an exact gate, so this adjudication does **not** remove or relax it after seeing outcomes. A future semantic contract excluding it would require an outcome-blind, main-approved freeze **before** any new population run; none is adopted here.

## Candidate and interpretation rule

`Ccompat` restores the exact Stage-A `response`, `score`/`score_derivatives`, coordinate method, checked `refit`, and accepted-residual summation, while retaining only a held bank conjugate and vectorized application of the identical duplicate predicate. It has passed small synthetic parity and independent static review, **not** N=1024 equivalence. The new full 1,024-frame balanced B/Ccompat/Cref run must keep exact discrete/counter/bit gates, continuous `2e−10/0`, historical Stage-A C comparison on all 1,024 Ccompat calls, current Cref comparison on 64 fixed frames, and first-failure stop. A successful run would support equivalence **for that registered run and exposed developmental population**, while the older cross-run discrepancy remains unresolved; it would not retroactively identify the old cause or prove universal bitwise determinism. A failed run is preserved and reported, without retry or tolerance tuning. Timing/FER claims are withheld until the full seal and subsequent truth gate.
