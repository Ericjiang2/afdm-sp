Implement the paper's SISO variance-prior GAMP with positive `−∂gS/∂P` and complex `ΦᴴS`. EM uses the paper's δ formula and corrected `β=(N+d−1)/(e+||y−Φμ||²+Σ_j V_j||φ_j||²)`: expected residual with Algorithm 3's declared diagonal covariance. Grid evolution uses the paper's top-Rbar δ set. Because its final grid update otherwise leaves μ at the previous grid, **charge a final GAMP posterior reconciliation** on the final grid, with no further EM/grid update. Full GESBL is a small-matrix synthetic reference only.

For each outer data pass, form the sensing matrix from the physical pilot plus its current hard-data feedback. The pilot pass uses zero data. Carry the current grid, δ, and β into the next data pass; each EM iteration resets GAMP messages to `μ=0, V=δ, S=0` on its current sensing matrix, and the charged final reconciliation does likewise. The grid step solves the simultaneous real bounded quadratic for all top-Rbar Doppler offsets (inactive offsets zero), with stable grid-index ties; it is not a sequential coordinate sweep. A GAMP cap hit is recorded even if the outer EM step proceeds. No state or warm-start choice depends on truth or decoder outcomes.

**F2E:** pass 1 uses pilot only; after each estimate charge the paper’s dense LMMSE on `y−H_eff pilot` with `(σ²/p_d)I=2σ²I`, since existing hard QPSK `{±.5±.5j}` has `p_d=0.5`. Hard-map each real/imaginary component to ±0.5 with zero tied positive; feed it to the next pass, up to four passes total. No early APP/LDPC. **F2T:** only on original F0+TF syndrome failures; its first F2 pass starts with hard QPSK data from the *already charged native APP*, then uses the same newly charged LMMSE feedback for later passes, also up to four. This initial-feedback substitution is a declared triggered specialization. Charge all new F2/continuation work.

Both policies pass the full top-155 `Channel` directly to the unchanged `scalar_joint` TF20/LDPC50 core. F2E uses two coded rounds, with the second retaining the geometry while continuing gains/check prior; F2E+CSTATE triggers only after its native syndrome. F2T gets the same bounded TF20/LDPC continuation as CSTATE. This omits F0 unique-delay and F1 `time_refine_keep_slots(max_paths=8)` front-end refits; those are explicit front-end differences, while detector/decoder core and cost clock stay common. No truncation or regrouping.

Before any real observation, qualify on fixed synthetic cases: (Q1) a small CPP/DAFT channel with two paths sharing a delay, comparing sensing columns, Doppler derivatives, adjoint, and operator application to direct dense construction (`rtol=atol=1e−10`); (Q2) complex full-GESBL posterior and corrected GAMP on a small well-conditioned matrix, checking finite positive variances, posterior residual and support, and reporting rather than forcing equality of diagonal and full covariance; (Q3) a full small EM→grid→final-reconciliation→top-Rbar pass with one hard-data feedback, checking finite objective, physically bounded grid, terminal effective-channel relative error ≤0.10 against the full-posterior reference, predictive residual relative error ≤0.10, and median diagonal-variance relative error ≤0.20; support differences are reported, because a diagonal covariance approximation need not preserve every low-energy rank; (Q4) identity-channel hard-data LMMSE with analytic shrink factor `1/(1+2σ²)`, error ≤1e−12, plus QPSK tie behavior; (Q5) synthetic interface passes `P>8` without path truncation into the unchanged TF input shape. Q3 is an engineering qualification on synthetic data, not a real-frame performance claim; failure stops qualification rather than retuning thresholds. Fixture dimensions, seeds, paths and thresholds are locked in `F2_QUALIFICATION.json` before execution.
