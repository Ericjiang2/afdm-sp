## Fixed acquisition-only subset and budget

Select exactly `s650_f0000` through `s650_f0007` by frame index, before inspecting their latency, outcome, or geometry. Bind each NPZ SHA and semantic observation hash through the original sealed population ledger and seal. Bind the reference `f1.py` SHA to the locked Stage-A registration (`c47c361d040436d1eb003543cbbdb66f6dc2b9813e1a5982ec48dfc2c2d5737c`) and verify the frozen source/baseline maps. The profiler takes only observed `y`, known pilot, `sigma2`, and source-built `N,c1,c2,lmax`; it never opens truth or final decoder outputs. Prior Stage-A timing for these IDs is an **observer-effect comparator**, not a new clock substituted into Stage B.

For each of eight frames, run one plain and one instrumented F1 call in the **same environment**, alternating `plain→profile` on even indices and `profile→plain` on odd indices to limit order bias. Each timing starts immediately before `PhysicalF1(...)` construction and ends after `.acquire(...)`, exactly matching `native_f1.receive_f1`'s `pilot_seconds` boundary; constructor time is separately reported. The eight instrumented calls use `cProfile` and inclusive explicit counters/timers on response, score/derivatives, ridge refit, duplicate check and coordinate search. Save every function's call count, self time and cumulative time; retain per-frame F1 counters and bank-build/total time. Run one **separate** frame-0 F1 acquisition under `tracemalloc` for Python-managed allocation sites; report its observer overhead separately and do not treat its peak as complete NumPy/RSS memory. Maximum: **17 F1-only acquisition calls, zero TF/LDPC/full wrappers, zero new observations**. Abort on any hash, numeric or geometry mismatch; do not retry a failed observed frame without a new explicit record. Expected plain F1 work is about `8×0.377≈3.0 s`; profiling overhead is unknown, soft expectation under two minutes and a **five-minute hard wall limit** on one CPU. No acquisition begins until this plan/source/input binding is frozen and reviewed.

The profile report will separate bank/CPP/phase construction, bank correlations and scoring, derivative/coordinate and cyclic work, refit/Gram/linear solves, duplicate/bookkeeping, NumPy dispatch and Python self time, temporary allocations/copies, and accidental serialization **where the profiler can actually distinguish them**. Nested explicit timers are **inclusive** and cannot be summed. NumPy dispatch versus native compute and cache misses may remain proxy measurements or **unclear**; no precise time is invented for an inseparable category. Classify each material cost as intrinsic algorithmic work, avoidable repetition, interpreter overhead, avoidable layout/allocation, reference artifact, or unclear. Do not infer an algorithmic complexity theorem from Python timings.

## Semantics frozen before optimization

The **algorithm** is exactly the Stage-A F1 physical bank and tie order, eight-slot budget, observed-pilot ridge score/refit, four safeguarded Newton steps per coordinate, eight insertion visits plus 72 cyclic visits at full slots, CPP/positive Doppler response, duplicate threshold, stopping/acceptance/condition checks, and ordered `(delay,ν,gain)` TF handoff. Candidate-bank construction, loop layout, immutable phase caching, vectorized equivalent operations and buffer reuse are implementation details only. No truth, decoder messages, altered grid/window, fewer visits, changed ridge/precision or approximate shortlist may enter F1-fast.

For later equivalence, compare exact ordered delay/path count, bank centers, stopping reason, branch/counter inventory and final bits; compare Dopplers, complex gains, objective-history values and TF-entry geometry with **absolute tolerance `2e−10`, relative tolerance `0`** (the already frozen Stage-A continuous-array parity tolerance). Numerical-failure classification and accepted/rejected discrete choices must match exactly. A changed material discrete choice or failed tolerance is a different comparator, not an optimized version of the same F1. First qualify synthetic cases; then all 1,024 archived observations and identical TF backend before any F1-fast risk/cost claim. Optimization choices are based on this fixed profile, not receiver outcomes.
