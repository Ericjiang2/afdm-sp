Source anchors below refer to `main.tex` inside [`literature/2607.18881v1_source.tar`](literature/2607.18881v1_source.tar), SHA-256 `f8b72ceac25eef234602d5ae985fbbec3f0959739af19ca1ef12637bc52986c4`. The only F2 source is the algebra-only `experiments/cstate_necessity_stageB_20260928/f2_algebra.py` and `f2_core.py`, which implement fixed-parameter Gaussian checks without EM/grid/feedback. Current physical/source counterparts are `experiments/sp_afdm_frozen_20260920/src/physical.py` (`daft`, `Channel.regressors/apply`) and `experiments/sp_afdm_native_joint_20260921/src/twofft.py` (`TimePathOps`, `scalar_joint`, `time_refine`); the coded F1 handoff is `experiments/cstate_necessity_stageA_20260928/native_f1.py:receive_f1`.
