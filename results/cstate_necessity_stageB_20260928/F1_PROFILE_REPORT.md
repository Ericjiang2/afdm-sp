# Frozen F1 acquisition profile (Stage B)

The source-bound AFDM run completed the preregistered eight-frame, 17-call F1-only profile: eight plain calls, eight cProfile/inclusive-timer calls interleaved by fixed frame ID, and one separate tracemalloc call. There were **zero full receiver, TF, LDPC, decoder, generator, or truth calls**. The exact observations, source hashes, call journal, and timing rows are in [F1_PROFILE_RAW.json](profile_run_v1/F1_PROFILE_RAW.json) and [F1_PROFILE_PLAN.md](F1_PROFILE_PLAN.md). No failed acquisition was retried.

The timed boundary matches the original `native_f1` pilot-acquisition boundary: `PhysicalF1` construction plus `acquire`, including AFDM transforms and bank creation. Across the eight uninstrumented calls, mean/median were **0.3588/0.3559 s** (range 0.3435–0.3855 s). Instrumented mean was **0.4196 s**, 16.9% higher; the instrumented times are for attribution, not an uninstrumented performance estimate. The separately recorded bank-build subinterval averaged **0.0162 s** (4.5% of plain mean). The historical Stage-A timing column is contextual only; it was not used to estimate profiler overhead.

Eight profiled calls covered 8 slots and 80 coordinate visits per frame. The separate tracemalloc pass recorded a peak of **86,644,520 bytes in Python-managed allocations**; it is not process RSS and does not quantify all native NumPy buffers. cProfile self/cumulative and inclusive timers are nested and nonadditive. NumPy dispatch versus native compute, cache misses, and CPU utilization within kernels remain **unclear** from this profile; no precision is imputed to those subdivisions.

The frozen cost labels are **A** intrinsic work, **B** avoidable repetition, **C** interpreter overhead, **D** allocation/data-layout overhead, **E** first-reference artifact, and **F** unresolved split. Path responses are A+B+C+D/E (the physical response is necessary, rebuilding its invariant shift/CPP/phase is not); the 2,575-item bank correlation is A with its BLAS/native split F. Coordinate scores/Newton first and second derivatives and cyclic visits are A, while their repeated array construction is B/D. Duplicate checks are A for the rule but C/E for 172,783 scalar Python invocations. Ridge Gram construction and linear solve are A; repeated conjugation is B/D/E. Loop bookkeeping is C, with no isolated cost beyond the nonadditive inclusive/cProfile rows. Cache misses and native NumPy compute are F. Raw/decision serialization occurs after acquisition and is **outside** this F1-only profile boundary; its isolated acquisition cost is zero by definition, while full-wrapper serialization remains to be measured in the matched run. These labels identify implementation opportunities, not an additive time decomposition.

`PhysicalF1Fast` therefore reuses each immutable delay/CPP/pilot base, holds the conjugated bank once, removes one repeated refit conjugation, and masks duplicate bank entries by the same `<1e-6` condition. It retains all 8 slots, 80 visits, 4 Newton steps per visit, score/ridge checks, tie order, and output schema. The optimization candidate and its synthetic equivalence gate are documented in [F1_FAST_EQUIVALENCE.md](F1_FAST_EQUIVALENCE.md). This profile does **not** establish a real-frame speedup or final decoded-bit equivalence; those require the separately frozen Stage-B matched run.
